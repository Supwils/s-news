### 7. Taiwan Cooking-Oil Benzopyrene Scandal Widens: TFDA Orders Two Product Classes Off Shelves by **July 6**
**Summary:** Per CNA and SETN on **July 5**, soybean salad oil from Chung Lien Oil was found to contain excess benzopyrene, with roughly **1,300** metric tons distributed downstream. The Taiwan FDA expanded a "precision recall" late on **July 4**: Category 1 covers bulk oils (salad oil, blended oil, etc.); Category 2 covers processed foods with **≥20%** of the affected oil (e.g., mayonnaise) — both due off shelves by **July 6** midnight. Products below **20%** must disclose use. Taichung health authorities re-inspected the plant on **July 5**, not ruling out high-moisture Brazilian soybeans or roasting contamination; Health Minister **Shih Chung-Liang** said production cannot resume until the cause is clarified. By **July 5**, **552** local businesses had been checked and delisted.

### 10. **354,957**-Person UK Biobank Study Links Coffee to Lower Cirrhosis, Liver Cancer, and Liver-Related Death
**Summary:** Per Cedars-Sinai and related coverage in **July**, a study in *Clinical Gastroenterology and Hepatology* followed **354,957** UK Biobank participants without baseline cirrhosis or liver cancer for a median **13** years. Versus non-drinkers, **≥5** cups/day associated with **32%** lower cirrhosis risk, **47%** lower liver cancer risk, and **42%** lower liver-related mortality; benefits appeared from **1–2** cups/day. MRI subcohort data showed less hepatic fat, iron, and fibro-inflammation; proteomics pointed to higher liver-synthesis/complement proteins and lower fibrosis markers. Caffeinated and decaf patterns were similar.
