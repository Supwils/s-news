### 7. Nature Aging: Long-term training reverses roughly half of age-related muscle molecular differences
**Summary:** A study published in **Nature Aging** on **July 3** analyzed skeletal muscle transcriptomics, lipidomics, and metabolomics in young and older adults with differing fitness, before and after submaximal acute exercise. At baseline, older adults showed lower expression of cellular respiration and energy metabolism genes versus similarly active young adults; **50%** of those age-related gaps were absent in trained older adults, whose profiles resembled younger adults. Acute exercise immune and stress responses in older adults correlated positively with fitness; multi-omic integration linked mitochondrial respiration, lipid metabolism, stress responses, and **NAD+** biology.

**Links:**

- [Nature Aging — Delayed molecular aging in exercise-trained human muscle (July 3, 2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-026-01150-x)

**Commentary:** Anti-aging evidence is moving from performance to molecules — consistent aerobic and resistance training from midlife onward may still rewrite muscle "biological age."

**Summary:** Per CNA and UDN on **July 3**, Chung Lien Oil's batch of roughly **1,300** tonnes of soybean salad oil (lot **315-1150404**, manufactured **April 4, 2026**) tested at **8.1 μg/kg** benzo[a]pyrene — **4×** the **2.0 μg/kg** legal limit; SGS reports also flagged three other PAHs. ## III. Nutrition & Diet

### 12. Dutch modeling study: Plant-protein shifts without planning risk nutrient shortfalls
**Summary:** Per News-Medical on **July 3**, a **Nutrients** dietary modeling study using **2019–2021** Dutch National Food Consumption Survey data (**3,570** participants) simulated replacing animal foods with plant alternatives. Both nutritionally conscious and less-conscious scenarios reduced total protein — largest drops in adults **71–79**; B vitamins, vitamin A, vitamin D, calcium, iron, iodine, selenium, and zinc fell across groups, especially among adolescents and women. Authors urge formulation, fortification, and age-tailored guidance — removing meat and dairy does not automatically equal adequacy.

## IV. Lifestyle

### 14. Sustained-release melatonin RCT: 28 days improved total sleep time and wellbeing
**Summary:** Per NutraIngredients on **July 3**, a double-blind RCT in **Clocks & Sleep** enrolled **59** healthy adults aged **30–60** with poor self-reported sleep for **28** nights of Melotime sustained-release melatonin (equivalent to **2 mg** melatonin) vs placebo. Polysomnography showed improved sleep efficiency and roughly **6.5%** more total sleep time versus baseline (about **6.5** extra hours across the study), faster sleep onset (~**10** minutes), and fewer night awakenings; subjective sleep quality improved from day **7**, psychological wellbeing from day **14**.

**Links:**

- [NutraIngredients — Sustained-release melatonin boosts sleep outcomes (July 3, 2026)](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2026/07/03/sustained-release-melatonin-may-boost-sleep-outcomes-for-poor-sleepers/)

**Commentary:** Melatonin isn't for everyone — CBT-I remains first-line for insomnia; screen for sleep apnea and other causes before supplementing.
