## II. Health & Medicine

### 6. Painless oral swab **qMIDS-V3** detects oral cancer in under an hour with **95.5%** accuracy
**Summary:** Per ScienceAlert on **July 8**, UK and Indian scientists tested the third-generation **quantitative Malignancy Index Diagnostic System (qMIDS-V3)** — a brush swab analyzing **4** oral-squamous-cell-carcinoma-linked mRNA markers (**INHBA, S100A16, YAP1, POLR2A**) with results in about **1 hour**. Across **1,090** brush biopsies from **545** patients, overall accuracy was **95.5%** with false-positive and false-negative rates below **5%**, published in **Biomarker Research**. Global oral cancer cases and deaths have more than doubled since **1990**; commercialization is underway with clinical use possible within **2 years**.

**Links:**

- [ScienceAlert — Painless swab detects oral cancers in an hour, with over 95% accuracy (July 8, 2026)](https://www.sciencealert.com/painless-swab-detects-oral-cancers-in-an-hour-with-over-95-accuracy)
- [Biomarker Research — qMIDS-V3 diagnostic study (2026)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40364-026-00963-7)

**Commentary:** Early oral cancer screening moving from invasive biopsy to rapid non-invasive testing matters especially for long-term smokers, drinkers, and those with persistent oral lesions.

### 7. Oral **GLP-1** drug **HRS-7535** hits Phase III goals: ~**10.9%** weight loss at **44** weeks
**Summary:** Per FierceBiotech and GlobeNewswire on **July 7–8**, Jiangsu Hengrui Pharmaceutical and Kailera Therapeutics announced Phase III success in China for oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist **HRS-7535** (Kailera code **KAI-7535**) in obesity and Type 2 diabetes. In the HARBOR-1 obesity trial, the **180 mg** arm averaged **10.9%** weight loss at **44 weeks** and **11.1%** at **50 weeks** (placebo ~**2.5%**); **2,000+** participants were enrolled. The OUTSTAND-2 diabetes trial cut **HbA1c** by up to **1.68%**, non-inferior to dapagliflozin. Nausea and vomiting were common; Kailera is running a global Phase II trial to optimize tolerability.

### 8. Copenhagen study: after weight loss, exercise improves vascular health more than medication alone
**Summary:** Per Medical Xpress and **Nature Metabolism** on **July 8**, University of Copenhagen researchers followed **130** people with severe obesity for one year after a low-calorie diet averaging **13.7 kg** lost, randomizing them to exercise, liraglutide, exercise plus drug, or placebo. The exercise arms (~**2.5 hours**/week of spinning and circuit training) saw **6%–7%** reductions in carotid artery wall thickness and lower inflammatory markers; drug-only and placebo groups showed no vascular improvement — medication helped maintain weight but not cardiovascular benefit. Published as a prespecified secondary analysis of the S-LiTE trial.

**Links:**

- [Medical Xpress — After weight loss, exercise improves cardiovascular health more than weight-loss medication (July 8, 2026)](https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-07-weight-loss-cardiovascular-health-medication.html)

**Commentary:** In the GLP-1 era, this reinforces "drugs for weight, exercise for the heart" — a modest weekly exercise dose can still move key vascular risk markers.

### 9. **UCSF** RCT: ketogenic diet may improve metabolic and psychiatric markers in psychotic disorders
**Summary:** Per News-Medical on **July 8**, a University of California, San Francisco team published in **Schizophrenia Bulletin** the first randomized controlled trial of a ketogenic diet in schizophrenia-spectrum or bipolar-1 disorder. After one month, the keto group showed significantly better metabolic markers than diet-as-usual controls; higher ketone levels correlated with lower depression scores (PHQ-9) independent of weight loss. An optional **4-month** single-arm extension (**25** participants) sustained metabolic gains with meaningful reductions in depression and schizophrenia symptoms plus cognitive improvement. NIMH partially funded the work.

**Links:**

## IV. Lifestyle

### 13. Harvard study: **90–120 minutes** of weekly strength training linked to longevity gains
**Summary:** Per AOL/Men's Journal on **July 8** citing Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health research in the **British Journal of Sports Medicine**, a **30-year** follow-up of nearly **147,000** adults found those doing ~**90–120** minutes of resistance training weekly had **13%** lower all-cause premature death risk, **19%** lower cardiovascular death risk, and **27%** lower neurodegenerative death risk versus non-lifters. Benefits were strongest when combined with regular aerobic exercise — aligning with U.S. guidelines of muscle-strengthening at least twice weekly across major muscle groups.

### 14. Glasgow study: each extra hour of **uninterrupted** sitting raises cancer death risk ~**9%**; light activity cuts it **12%**
**Summary:** Per Science and Technology Daily relayed by China Economic Net on **July 8**, **PLOS Medicine** analyzed **91,292** UK Biobank participants with **7** days of accelerometry and **12.38** years of follow-up. Researchers distinguished "continuous sedentary time" (≥**30** min bouts with ≥**90%** sitting) from interrupted sitting. Continuous sitting linked to higher cancer mortality, total cancer incidence, obesity-related cancers, and Type 2 diabetes-related cancers; replacing **1 hour** of sitting with light activity cut cancer death risk **12%**. Authors argue guidelines should track bout pattern, not just total sedentary hours.

**Links:**

- [China Economic Net / Science and Technology Daily — Continuous sitting raises cancer death risk (July 8, 2026)](http://www.ce.cn/xwzx/shgj/gdxw/202607/t20260708_3074394.shtml)

**Commentary:** Office workers and long-distance drivers can set "stand up **2** minutes every **30**" micro-breaks — anti-cancer benefit may rival a single weekend hard session.
