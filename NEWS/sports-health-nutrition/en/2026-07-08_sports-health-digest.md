### 5. **Frontiers** study: **low-volume** Nordic hamstring training beats high volume for youth soccer change-of-direction
**Summary:** A **Frontiers in Sports and Active Living** paper published **July 3** randomized **60** pubertal soccer players into low (**4×3**), moderate (**4×6**), or high (**4×9**) Nordic curl sessions over **8** in-season weeks. Low volume produced significantly better change-of-direction performance — with and without the ball — than moderate or high volume (effect sizes **d = 0.56–0.78**). Low and moderate doses also outperformed high volume on **10 m** sprints. Authors conclude a "less is more" hamstring dose may be optimal in-season.

**Links:**

- [Frontiers — Low-volume Nordic hamstring training improves change-of-direction performance (July 3, 2026)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2026.1818507/full)

**Commentary:** Amateur footballers and runners can adopt weekly low-rep Nordic curls for hamstring injury prevention — but progress gradually from low reps.

### 6. Painless oral swab **qMIDS** detects oral cancer in under an hour with **>95%** accuracy
**Summary:** Per ScienceAlert on **July 8**, UK and Indian scientists tested the **quantitative Malignancy Index Diagnostic System (qMIDS)** — a brush swab analyzing **4** oral-cancer-linked mRNA markers with results in about **1 hour**. In **95** subjects, overall accuracy was **95.5%** with false-positive and false-negative rates below **5%**. Global oral cancer cases and deaths have more than doubled since **1990**, linked to smoking, alcohol, sugary drinks, and HPV. Commercialization is underway with clinical use possible within **2 years**.

**Links:**

**Commentary:** Early oral cancer screening moving from invasive biopsy to rapid non-invasive testing matters especially for long-term smokers, drinkers, and those with persistent oral lesions.

### 10. **IARC/WHO**-led **EPIC** study: ultra-processed foods tied to blood "bad lipid" metabolic signature
**Summary:** Per Technology Networks and Medical Xpress on **July 6–7**, IARC's Jessica Blanco-Lopez team analyzed diet and blood metabolomics from **15,200** EPIC participants, publishing in **Critical Reviews in Food Science and Nutrition**. Higher ultra-processed food intake correlated with **22** circulating metabolites — including biomarkers of impaired fatty-acid oxidation and mitochondrial dysfunction — and lower levels of lipids essential for membrane stability. Plasma stearic-acid and long-chain PUFA patterns suggest harm may extend beyond fat content, with excess carbs stimulating endogenous lipid synthesis.

### 12. Glasgow study: each extra hour of **uninterrupted** sitting raises cancer death risk ~**9%**; light activity cuts it **12%**
**Summary:** Per Science and Technology Daily relayed by China Economic Net on **July 8**, **PLOS Medicine** analyzed **91,292** UK Biobank participants with **7** days of accelerometry and **12.38** years of follow-up. Continuous sitting linked to higher cancer mortality, total cancer incidence, obesity-related cancers, and Type 2 diabetes-related cancers; replacing **1 hour** of sitting with light activity cut cancer death risk **12%**. Authors argue guidelines should track bout pattern, not just total sedentary hours.
