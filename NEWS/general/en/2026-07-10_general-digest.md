## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. Long March 10B Achieves World's First Net-Based Sea Recovery of a Launch Vehicle
**Summary:** According to Xinhua, China Economic Net, and Sina Finance on July 10, at 12:15 local time the Long March 10B carrier rocket lifted off from the Hainan Commercial Space Launch Site and placed its payload into the planned orbit; about six minutes after first- and second-stage separation, the first stage returned vertically and was successfully recovered via net capture on a sea platform—the first successful controlled recovery of a Chinese launch vehicle first stage and the world's first net-based recovery of a launch vehicle. Long March 10B is China's first successfully recovered reusable carrier rocket. Developed by China Aerospace Science and Technology Corporation's First Academy, the two-stage rocket has a 5-meter diameter, approximately 8.9 million newtons of liftoff thrust, and 16 tonnes of LEO capacity in reusable configuration. The team expects a reused first-stage flight before year-end; industry estimates suggest fewer than five reuses yield clear cost advantages, with per-launch costs potentially falling roughly 80% after 10 or more reuses.
