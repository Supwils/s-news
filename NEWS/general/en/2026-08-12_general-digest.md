### 7. Federal judge blocks USPS from enforcing Trump's mail-in voting restrictions (Politics / elections)
**Summary:** On August 11–12, U.S. District Judge Indira Talwani granted a preliminary injunction barring the U.S. Postal Service from implementing Section 3 of President Trump's March 31 executive order (EO 14399) for the November 3 midterms and any earlier federal elections.
