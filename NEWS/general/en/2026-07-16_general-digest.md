## V. Other Regions and Global Opinion

### 11. Pew survey: in 25 of 36 countries, China is now viewed more favorably than the U.S.—a first in roughly two decades of tracking
**Summary:** A Pew Research Center study released Jul 15 and covered by AP and BBC into Jul 16 surveyed more than 42,000 adults across 36 countries from February to May 2026. In 25 countries, more people viewed China favorably than the United States; only six—including Japan, India, South Korea, the Philippines, Poland, and Israel—still preferred the U.S. In 22 countries, confidence in Xi Jinping exceeded confidence in Donald Trump. Researchers attributed the flip to both improving views of China and deteriorating views of the U.S., with fieldwork overlapping the U.S.–Israel war on Iran. Respondents still more often said the U.S. government respects personal freedoms, but that gap has narrowed; a higher median share said the U.S. interferes in other countries’ affairs a great deal or a fair amount than said the same of China.
