### 2. DR Congo and M23 agree on roadmap for comprehensive peace talks as fighting and Ebola persist (Africa)
**Summary:** The Democratic Republic of the Congo and the AFC/M23 rebel coalition said in a Saturday joint statement that they have agreed on a sequenced, time-bound negotiating roadmap under the Doha framework, plus a standardized method for reporting ceasefire violations and a mechanism to handle implementation disputes. The roadmap followed five days of talks in Switzerland from August 17–21 with representatives from the United States, Qatar, Togo, Switzerland, and the African Union Commission; a ceasefire verification mission is due Monday in Minembwe, South Kivu. Fighting continues: an M23 spokesman alleged government kamikaze-drone strikes on populated areas Saturday. The UN has warned that drone use is further destabilizing the east, and an Ebola outbreak since May has killed about 2,500 people. The conflict, reignited in 2021, has displaced an estimated 5.3 million.

**Summary:** After U.S. 50% tariffs on about $20 billion of Canadian goods took effect early Saturday, Prime Minister Mark Carney announced in Ottawa that Canada will match them “dollar for dollar” starting September 8 (the Tuesday after Labor Day), targeting U.S. steel, dairy, appliances, farm equipment, pulp and paper, and electronics, with full lists due in coming days and multi-year industry support next week. Two-way goods and services trade runs about $880 billion a year; the Business Roundtable warned of higher costs for firms and households on both sides.
