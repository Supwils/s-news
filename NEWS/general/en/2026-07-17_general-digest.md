## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. Xi addresses World AI Conference: four governance principles, 5,000 AI training slots for developing countries (Policy)
**Summary:** Xinhua reported on July 17 that President Xi Jinping attended the opening of the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference and High-level Meeting on Global AI Governance in Shanghai, delivering a keynote on building a just and equitable global AI governance system.
