### 3. Some 50,000–60,000 migrants enter Ceuta in about 24 hours; Spain deploys troops (Europe / Migration)

## III. China Policy and Economy

### 6. China’s July manufacturing PMI falls to 49.2, back in contraction after four months (Data)
**Summary:** China’s National Bureau of Statistics said on July 31 that the manufacturing PMI fell to 49.2 in July, down 1.1 points from June and back below 50 after four months; the non-manufacturing business activity index was 49.0 and the composite PMI output index 49.3. Officials and analysts pointed to extreme heat, rainstorms, typhoons, and the traditional production off-season. Equipment and high-tech manufacturing stayed in expansion at 51.4 and 53.3, respectively, and the manufacturing expectations index held at 54.1, signaling relatively firmer new drivers and sentiment.
