### 8. Three Ministries Announce 2027 Adjustments to Energy-Saving and NEV Vehicle Tax Incentives
**Summary:** Per People's Daily, Sina Finance, and other outlets July 3–5, the Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration, and MIIT jointly announced that from January 1, 2027, halved vehicle and vessel tax for energy-saving cars will end, as will exemptions for pure-electric commercial vehicles, plug-in (including range-extended) hybrid commercial vehicles, and fuel-cell commercial vehicles; the rules apply to both existing and newly acquired vehicles. Authorities said 2025 NEV domestic sales reached 16.49 million units, exceeding 50% market share, and continued blanket exemptions would weaken tax fairness and regulatory function. Pure-electric and fuel-cell passenger vehicles remain outside the vehicle tax scope and are unaffected; plug-in passenger vehicle owners may face roughly RMB 300–540 more per year depending on provincial rates.
