### 4. Canada unveils about $20 billion in retaliatory tariffs, effective September 8 (Trade)
**Summary:** On Tuesday, August 25, Canada formally announced retaliatory tariffs on about $20 billion of U.S. goods, saying the duties match "dollar for dollar" the 50% U.S. tariffs President Donald Trump imposed over the weekend on a similar value of Canadian products. Ottawa's list covers more than 700 U.S. goods at rates of 15%–50%, spanning dairy, seafood, appliances, wood and paper, and clothing; tariffs on U.S. steel and aluminum rise to 50%. Trump attacked Canada on Truth Social as "the most difficult and unreasonable" and floated renaming Lake Ontario "Lake America." Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc told CNBC Ottawa still prefers a deal but is "not waiting by the phone."
