### 2. Himalayan flood death toll nears 1,000 with over 4,000 missing as tunnel rescues drag on (Disaster)
**Summary:** As of Aug. 31, authorities put combined deaths from last week’s Nepal–China border glacial collapse and flash floods at about 955 (roughly 939 in Nepal, 16 in China) and missing at about 4,471 (about 3,925 in Nepal, 546 in China). China reported more than 2,100 rescue personnel and at least 220 million yuan in relief; Xi Jinping raised missing-person searches and secondary-disaster risks while meeting Kyrgyzstan’s president in Bishkek.

### 4. House returns: stopgap funding vote first to avert a Sept. 30 shutdown (Politics)
**Summary:** The House returned Monday from a roughly five-week recess with a Senate-passed continuing resolution—funding the government into early December, around Dec. 11—atop the agenda to avoid a shutdown before the Sept. 30 fiscal deadline. The Senate cleared the bipartisan measure 90–6, with language temporarily closing a Border Patrol funding-transfer loophole Democrats flagged and briefly restraining political appointees from canceling federal grants. House GOP leaders plan to fast-track it under suspension of the rules (two-thirds required). Messaging votes condemning socialism are also expected; after two Democratic special-election winners are sworn in, the GOP’s majority shrinks to about two seats.
