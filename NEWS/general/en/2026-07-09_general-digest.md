## IV. U.S.–China Relations

### 11. Public Comment Period for U.S.–China Board of Trade Closes Tonight
**Summary:** According to TechTimes and an official USTR Federal Register notice, the deadline for public comments on bilateral trade optimization under the U.S.–China Board of Trade framework is 11:59 p.m. ET on July 10. The mechanism stems from outcomes of Trump's May visit to Beijing and aims to coordinate tariff adjustments on "non-sensitive product" categories through a government-to-government channel; USTR says it could cover roughly $30 billion in imports. The process runs: public comments → USTR selects a U.S. negotiating list → bilateral negotiations on product-level tariff modifications → implementation. Unlike the prior Section 301 exclusion process where importers petitioned individually, the Board of Trade works through negotiated product-category lists—making this comment period the only direct opportunity for businesses to influence USTR's negotiating table. USTR data show the U.S. goods trade deficit with China fell 46% year-over-year in the post-summit adjustment period.
