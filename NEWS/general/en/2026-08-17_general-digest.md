### 2. Indonesia Flores quake death toll rises to 68 as Independence Day aid still struggles to reach cut-off villages (disaster)

**Summary:** Indonesian disaster officials said the death toll from Saturday’s magnitude-7.7 earthquake in East Nusa Tenggara, centered near Flores, rose from about 54 to at least 68, with more than 200 injured; AP cited BNPB figures of 213 injured and some 5,000 people in temporary shelters. The U.S. Geological Survey put the depth at about 10 km, shortly before 6 a.m. Saturday local time; a tsunami warning was later lifted. Authorities recorded 1,576 aftershocks into Monday. More than 1,300 homes were damaged in the province and nearly 250 destroyed on Flores; the governor declared a 14-day emergency on Sunday. Monday was Indonesia’s 81st Independence Day; President Prabowo Subianto led a moment of silence in Jakarta. Nearly 1,500 military and police personnel and 10 aircraft were moving about 275 tons of aid, but landslides still isolated some communities.
