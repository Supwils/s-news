### 1. Magnitude 7.1 quake hits Japan’s Kumamoto; mall collapse leaves dozens feared trapped (Disaster)
**Summary:** At 4:27 p.m. local time on July 28, a major earthquake struck Kumamoto Prefecture on Kyushu—magnitude 7.1 by Japan’s Meteorological Agency (about 6.8 by the USGS), with maximum seismic intensity 7. The second floor of an Aeon mall in Kashima collapsed after an explosion, with roughly 20–30 employees unaccounted for; at least one person was killed and dozens injured, while about 48,000 households lost power and over 10,500 lost water. Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi convened an emergency task force; TSMC, Sony, and Fujifilm evacuated plants, while Honda and Toyota paused some operations. A tsunami advisory was later lifted, and officials warned of strong aftershocks over the coming week.
