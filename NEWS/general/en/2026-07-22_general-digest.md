### 3. Trump approves a U.S.–Saudi civilian nuclear pact that may allow uranium enrichment (Diplomacy/Nonproliferation)
**Summary:** AP and BBC reported on July 22, citing people familiar with the matter, that President Trump has approved a roughly 30-year U.S.–Saudi civilian nuclear cooperation agreement (a “123 Agreement”), expected to be announced as early as Wednesday and sent to Congress; after a joint study, it could allow a uranium enrichment facility in Saudi Arabia with U.S. firms helping build the civilian program. The deal is not expected to include the IAEA Additional Protocol or the UAE-style “gold standard” of forgoing enrichment and reprocessing, and it is not contingent on Saudi–Israel normalization.
