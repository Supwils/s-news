### 3. Colombia under emergency after 7.4 quake; death toll at least 132 (Latin America / disaster)

## II. U.S. Politics and Economy

### 5. Trump signs childhood-vaccine order, cutting universal recommendations from 18 diseases to 11 (Politics / public health)
**Summary:** On August 10, President Trump signed an executive order recognizing “Gold Standard Childhood Vaccine Recommendations,” reducing diseases recommended for all children from 18 (CDC 2024 framing) to 11, with others shifted to shared clinical decision-making. The order also recommends splitting the combined MMR vaccine into separate measles, mumps, and rubella shots and spacing immunizations across separate visits. The White House said the move aligns the U.S. with peer developed countries and expands parental choice; it follows HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.’s earlier schedule overhaul that a court blocked, and directs the Justice Department to challenge state mandate laws conflicting with parental, religious, and related rights. The shift arrives amid measles pressure and the back-to-school season.

### 10. Typhoon Chan-hom nears eastern Japan with strong wind and rain warnings (Asia-Pacific / weather)
**Summary:** Japan’s weather agency said on August 11 that Typhoon Chan-hom, the season’s 15th, was east of Iwaki, Fukushima, moving west with central pressure near 985 hPa and maximum gusts around 126 kph, with possible landfall in the Kanto–Tohoku Pacific belt that afternoon or night. A blocking high over northern Japan steered the unusual westbound track during the Obon holiday travel peak.
