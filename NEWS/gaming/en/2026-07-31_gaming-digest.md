### 1. Sony Doubles Down on Ending PlayStation Discs in 2028; Retail May Shift to Code-in-a-Box (Platform)

**Summary:** On July 31, during its earnings Q&A, Sony addressed backlash over stopping physical disc production for new PlayStation games from January 2028. CFO Lin Tao said the main driver is industry-wide digitalization and that Sony will “cautiously move this forward,” while saying it has not yet seen a clear negative impact on the business. She added that North America already sells packages with a code and no disc, and that Sony is talking with regional retail partners for win-win outcomes; differentiation versus PC, she argued, rests more on curated content, a stable environment, and affordability than on discs themselves.

### 3. Xbox Tech Chief: Recent Outage Should Not Have Blocked Disc Play; Fix Incoming (Platform)

**Summary:** After the roughly day-long Xbox network outage around July 26–27 that left some players unable to launch even physical discs, Xbox technology chief Scott Van Vliet told media that an external licensing-service failure left some consoles unable to use saved entitlements correctly. By design, he said, disc-based entitlement checks should not stop players during an outage or offline; he called the situation unacceptable and said a fix will ship in an upcoming update. The comments land amid heightened debate over physical media and Sony’s disc phase-out.
