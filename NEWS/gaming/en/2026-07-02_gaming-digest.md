## III. Regulation & M&A

### 8. Bangladesh Enacts Gambling Prevention Act 2026, Targeting Online Betting and Crypto Flows

**Summary:** On July 2, Bangladesh state news agency BSS reported that the Gambling Prevention Act, 2026 took effect July 1 after presidential assent and gazette publication, replacing the 1867 Public Gambling Act. It defines online gambling, sports betting, virtual casinos, VPN gambling, cryptocurrency transactions, match-fixing, and related digital offenses. Operating online platforms carries up to seven years' imprisonment and a Tk 50 million fine; general online gambling offenses up to five years or Tk 10 million; organized gambling and crypto-linked laundering up to ten years. The law authorizes AI, deep packet inspection, transaction monitoring, and analytics to detect gambling, and allows confiscation of bank accounts, digital wallets, servers, domains, and related assets.

- [BSS — Bangladesh enacts new anti-online gambling law (Jul 2)](https://www.bssnews.net/news/401895)

**Commentary:** Another South Asian market legislates against digital betting rails — World Cup season plus crypto gambling makes enforcement tech part of the statute itself.
