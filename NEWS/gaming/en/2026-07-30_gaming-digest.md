## I. M&A and Industry Data

### 1. EA Clears All Regulatory Approvals for $55B Saudi-Led Buyout; Close Eyed for Aug 4 (M&A)

**Summary:** Electronic Arts filed an 8-K with the U.S. SEC on July 30 stating that, as of that date, all regulatory approvals required to complete its merger with Oak-Eagle had been obtained. The company currently expects the deal to close on or about the close of trading on August 4, 2026, subject to remaining customary closing conditions. The buyer consortium comprises Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF), Silver Lake, and Jared Kushner’s Affinity Partners in an all-cash transaction valued at about $55 billion—among the largest leveraged buyouts on record. After closing, EA is expected to remain headquartered in Redwood City, California, with CEO Andrew Wilson continuing to lead.

**Summary:** On July 30, the Game Publishing Committee of the China Audio-video and Digital Publishing Association released the China Gaming Industry Report for January–June 2026 at a ChinaJoy / CDEC forum in Shanghai.
