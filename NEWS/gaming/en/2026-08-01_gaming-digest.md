**Summary:** The worldwide Xbox console price update Microsoft announced on Xbox Wire on June 25 takes effect on August 1, 2026: roughly +$100 for 512GB models, +$150 for 1TB models, with the 2TB SKU discontinued. ### 2. Supply chain: AMD notifies board partners of ~10%+ Radeon GPU+memory kit hikes in August (Hardware)

**Summary:** VideoCardz, TweakTown, and others cite channel reports that AMD has notified AIB partners that Radeon GPU and memory (including GDDR6) kit prices will rise by at least about 10% starting in August.
