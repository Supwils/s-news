## II. Regulation, Product Rules & Streaming

### 5. GTA 6 Japan physical download codes expire 170 days after launch (Regulation)

**Summary:** GamesIndustry.biz and IGN, citing Rockstar’s support page, confirmed that Japanese PS5 “physical” editions of Grand Theft Auto VI contain download codes—not discs—that expire 170 days after the November 19, 2026 launch (around May 8, 2027). Coverage links the window to Japan’s Payment Services Act rules on prepaid digital goods, under which sub-six-month expiry can reduce deposit obligations. PSN digital purchases are unaffected; Xbox physical editions are not described as locked the same way.

**Links:**
