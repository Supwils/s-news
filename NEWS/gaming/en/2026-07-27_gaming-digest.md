### 9. Wemade’s WEMIX$ stablecoin hacked via admin privileges; peg crashes to about $0.0008 (Asia)

**Summary:** On the evening of Jul 26, an attacker seized administrator privileges on the WEMIX Dollar (WEMIX$) contract and minted roughly 5.22 million tokens, which were swapped and bridged out. Wemade / the Wemix Foundation suspended bridges, liquidity pools, and related game-ecosystem services while working with exchanges to freeze assets. Seoul Economic Daily, Chosun Biz, and Inven Global reported on Jul 27, 2026; it is the ecosystem’s second major security incident in recent years.

**Links:**
