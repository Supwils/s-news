## II. Market & Platforms

### 4. Circana: US gaming spend fell 21% in June 2026; hardware -62%, H1 only 1% behind (Market)

**Summary:** Circana’s latest monthly tracker put US video-game spending at about $4.5 billion in June 2026, down roughly 21% from about $5.7 billion a year earlier. Hardware spending fell about 62% to roughly $383 million against Switch 2’s record June 2025 launch month; content was down about 12% and accessories about 21%. Subscriptions rose about 7%—the only tracked segment in growth. Xbox Series was the only console hardware line up year-on-year (more than double), while Switch 2 still led units and dollars for the month and year-to-date. First-half spending was about $27.5 billion, only ~1% behind 2025. Eurogamer, GamesIndustry.biz, and GamesBeat reported the figures.
