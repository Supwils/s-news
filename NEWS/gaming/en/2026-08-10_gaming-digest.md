### 9. India’s Supreme Court advances final-hearing track on nationwide real-money online gaming ban (regulation)

**Summary:** August 10 reporting says India’s Supreme Court directed parties to complete pleadings in constitutional challenges to the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025, moving the dispute toward final hearings. The law, in force since May 1, 2026, bans online real-money games nationwide regardless of skill or chance, while carving out esports and qualifying social games; the ban remains in effect until a merits ruling.

**Links:**

- [Gambling Talk — Supreme Court moves toward final hearings on online gaming ban](https://gamblingtalk.net/news/indias-supreme-court-moves-toward-final-hearings-on-nationwide-online-gaming-ban)
