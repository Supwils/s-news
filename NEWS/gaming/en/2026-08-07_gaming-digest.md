### 4. PIF-led consortium completes ~$55B take-private of Electronic Arts (M&A)

**Summary:** Electronic Arts said a consortium of Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake, and Affinity Partners completed its acquisition—about $55 billion in total value—with shareholders receiving $210 cash per share and the stock delisted from Nasdaq. The deal was announced in September 2025, approved by stockholders in December 2025, and closed around Aug. 4, 2026, with follow-on coverage continuing on Aug. 7. It ranks among the largest gaming acquisitions after Microsoft–Activision Blizzard; CEO Andrew Wilson remains in place.
