## II. Regulation, Litigation, and Streaming

### 6. Sony and Microsoft Move to Dismiss Console Tariff-Refund Class Actions; Panic Already Refunded Buyers (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per Kotaku and TechTimes on September 3, Sony (around September 1) and Microsoft (earlier) asked federal courts to dismiss suits seeking a share of government tariff refunds after 2025 IEEPA tariffs were struck down. Sony's counsel argued buying a console at the advertised price is "not a legally cognizable injury in fact"; Microsoft said there is "nothing unjust" about getting what was paid for. Coverage notes Sony expects roughly ¥80 billion (~$508 million) in related refunds this fiscal year. By contrast, Playdate maker Panic automatically returned $12.36 to every customer who paid a labeled tariff surcharge. Nintendo faces a similar case; judges have not ruled.
