### 2. Valve Open-Sources Steam Machine E-Ink Accessory "Inkterface"

**Summary:** On July 3, The Verge, GamingOnLinux, and The Byte Report reported that Valve published the full design for the Steam Machine's front e-ink display on SteamOS GitLab under the MIT license, officially naming it "Inkterface" (internally once called Mango Frunk). The release includes 3D-printable CAD files, PCB layouts, firmware, a bill of materials, and an assembly video; builders must source parts such as an Adafruit ESP32 and e-ink panel themselves. Valve confirmed it will not manufacture or sell the accessory, though third parties like JSAUX have signaled commercial versions; a future Steam app will connect via Bluetooth to show system stats or custom artwork.
