### 6. Philippines PAGCOR Orders Foreign-Partner Disclosures and Launches Unified Venue License

**Summary:** On July 10, Manila Bulletin reported that the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR) requires all licensed gaming corporations to formally declare exclusive partnerships with foreign gaming partners, with supporting documents due within 30 days of a July 6 notice. The same day, PAGCOR's board approved a Unified Gaming License (UGL) as an alternative venue licensing path: a ₱1.2 million application fee, three-year validity, and minimum requirements of 25 electronic bingo and 25 electronic casino terminals plus sports betting and poker offerings, aiming to standardize nationwide electronic gaming venue expansion.
