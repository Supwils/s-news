### 2. gamescom Public Days Open as German President Steinmeier Makes Historic Visit (Trade Show / Culture)
**Summary:** Per tagesschau and Inven Global on Aug 27, gamescom 2026 opened fully to the public (through Aug 30), and Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier became the first German head of state to visit the show, delivering the gamescom congress keynote. He noted Germany has more than 40 million players and that games are "already at the center of society," calling them culturally meaningful—and therefore meaningful for democracy—while warning about addiction (recognized by the WHO), glorified violence, and extremist content, and urging clear rules. This year's congress themes are "Playing Field Democracy" and "The Games Effect."

### 7. Philippines' PAGCOR: No New Online Gaming Licenses for Now; Licensees Down to 48 (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per Philstar on Aug 27, PAGCOR Chairman Alejandro Tengco told a House budget hearing that the moratorium on new online gaming licenses in place since March 2024 will remain, with no plan to lift it yet as the industry consolidates. Active online gaming licensees have fallen from 74 to 48; fee rates of 30% for e-game operators and 25% for integrated resorts will not change immediately. Tengco also said PAGCOR fined an operator P1 million and ordered it to stop a recent influencer phone giveaway promotion.

**Links:**

- [Philstar — Pagcor rules out entry of new e-gaming operators](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/08/27/2551995/pagcor-rules-out-entry-new-e-gaming-operators)

**Commentary:** A "exit-only" licensing stance keeps squeezing Southeast Asia's online gaming supply while compliance consolidation comes first.
