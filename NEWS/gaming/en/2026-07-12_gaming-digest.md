## II. Industry Shocks & Regulatory Markets

### 5. Quebec Gaming Industry in Limbo as Layoffs, Tax Credits, and AI Uncertainty Converge

**Summary:** CBC reported on July 12 that Quebec's video game sector is in deep post-pandemic correction. The province hosts 300+ studios and roughly 45% of Canada's game developers, generating over CAD 1.4 billion annually — yet Epic Montreal cut 1,000+ roles, Ubisoft's ~4,000-person Montreal hub was hit, and Eidos, EA, Behaviour Interactive, and others announced layoffs over the past four months. The Quebec Video Game Guild estimates ~1,000 jobs lost in six months. Meanwhile, provincial wage tax credits were cut by about one-third starting in 2024, with a rising non-refundable share from 2025 onward; AI's long-term impact on hiring remains unclear.
