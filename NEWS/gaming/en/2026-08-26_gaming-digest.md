## II. Regulation & Platforms

### 8. Roblox Restricts "Reward-Driven Media Feeds" on Kids and Select Accounts (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per GamesIndustry.biz, PocketGamer.biz, and Roblox's DevForum on Aug 26, Roblox enacted an immediate policy barring games in Roblox Kids and Select that combine short-form media feeds with autoplay, infinite scroll, or looping mechanics plus rewards—or implied rewards—for continued viewing. User-initiated Rewarded Video Ads remain exempt due to rate limits and clear end points. Roblox said continuous reward-driven consumption is inappropriate for younger users; Philippines DICT separately noted ongoing coordination with Roblox on child safety the same day.

### 9. Philippines Formally Blocks Gorebox After Mutual Agreement with Developer (Regulation)
