### 1. Steam “Teraleak” dumps ~12TB of 2003–2013 builds, including third-party betas (Security)
**Summary:** According to Video Games Chronicle, GamesRadar+, and Kotaku on August 30, roughly 12–13TB of Steam uploads spanning 2003–2013 is circulating online, dubbed the Steam2 Teraleak.
