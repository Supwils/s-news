### 10. EU MiCA Takes Effect July 1; Only One of Three Major Blockchain-Gaming Payment Processors Cleared

**Summary:** Around July 6, GAMES.GG analyzed EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation effective July 1 and its impact on blockchain gaming payments.
