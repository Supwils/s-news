### 7. Apple TV U.S. price hike: $14.99 monthly; Apple One Individual also rises (streaming)
**Summary:** CNBC and Variety reported on August 28 that Apple raised U.S. Apple TV pricing effective immediately to $14.99 per month (from $12.99) and $119 per year (from $99). The Apple One Individual tier—bundling Apple TV, Music, iCloud+, and Arcade—rose from $19.95 to $21.95 monthly. Existing subscribers get about a month’s notice before the new rate is charged. It is the fourth hike in four years, tripling the $4.99 launch price from 2019; Apple TV remains one of the few major streamers without an ad tier.
