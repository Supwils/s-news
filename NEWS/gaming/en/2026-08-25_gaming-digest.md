## II. Market Data & Investment

### 6. Newzoo: Global Games Market to Hit $213.9bn in 2026, Up 6.1% YoY (Market)
**Summary:** Per GamesIndustry.biz on Aug 25, Newzoo projects $213.9 billion in global games revenue for 2026, up 6.1% year on year, with the full Global Games Market Report due Sept 10. China and the U.S. will account for more than half of consumer spending; consoles are forecast at 22% ($46.9bn, +5.1%), mobile at 57% ($121.1bn, +6.8%), and PC at 21% ($45.9bn, +5.3%). The November launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 is expected to lift full-game spending by 17.5%—the strongest growth of any business model. Player growth is set to slow toward a 3.2% CAGR through 2029, when the global player base reaches about 4.07 billion, with future gains depending more on retention and monetization than new-user acquisition.
