## II. Regulation & China Market

### 6. India's ED announces fresh Gameskraft attachment of about Rs 1,906 crore; cumulative case total near Rs 2,401 crore (Regulation)

**Summary:** India's Enforcement Directorate said on Jul 24 it has provisionally attached assets worth about Rs 1,906 crore linked to online real-money gaming firm Gameskraft and related parties under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (action dated Jul 22), covering deposits, funds, equity, and multiple properties tied to apps including RummyCulture, RummyPrime, Playship, and RummyTime. ED alleged a user base on the order of 30 million, 10%–15% platform commissions, BOT opponents, and practices that encouraged repeated wagering; cumulative attachment/seizure/freezing in the case is now about Rs 2,401 crore. Online money gaming was banned in India in August 2025.
