### 5. CD Projekt shrinks Project Sirius again, from 83 developers to about 65 (Staffing)

**Summary:** Mid-August coverage cited a company statement that The Witcher multiplayer experiment Project Sirius laid off nine people (four in Boston, five in Warsaw) and invited nine others to internal hiring, taking the team from 83 as of April 30 to about 65. CD Projekt said it “adjusted the size of the Sirius team to reflect the project’s needs at this stage.” The title was revealed in 2022, rebooted in 2023 with 29 layoffs, and still has no date; The Witcher 4 continues to employ most of the company.
