## II. Shows, Awards & Security

### 4. Multiple Gamescom exhibitors report stolen gear; organizers urge insurance and optional booth security (Show)
**Summary:** Per VGC, TheGamer, and Inven Global on August 29, at least three exhibitors reported overnight thefts: solo indie Ryan Laley (Mimic) lost laptops and spoke through tears; publisher iam8bit lost two demo laptops for Petal Runner and Escape Academy 2 and feared unfinished builds could leak; Tessera Studios found its Indie Area cabinet forced open, losing two laptops and a Steam Deck. Gamescom said it is aware, cases are with police, venue-wide guards are in place, and individual stand security plus theft insurance are available under show terms—drawing criticism that the response shifts blame onto exhibitors.
