### 2. UK high-street chain GAME Retail enters administration with about £15.8 million in debts (Retail)

**Summary:** On July 20, 2026, GamesHub and others reported that GAME Retail Limited—the UK’s last major high-street gaming retailer—has entered administration. James Saunders and Lauren Wentworth of KR8 Advisory were appointed administrators in April; at appointment, total debts stood at about £15.8 million, including roughly £3.5 million owed to secured creditors, with the remaining ~£12 million for unsecured creditors unlikely to see meaningful recovery. Frasers Group, owned by Mike Ashley, still owns the GAME brand, which could survive in some form—possibly folded further into Sports Direct’s gaming e-commerce—subject to administrators’ recommendations.

### 6. Federal judge issues 14-day TRO pausing Paramount’s ~$111 billion Warner Bros. Discovery takeover (M&A)

**Summary:** On July 20, 2026, U.S. District Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín granted a temporary restraining order sought by a coalition of 12 states led by California AG Rob Bonta, pausing Paramount from closing its roughly $111 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Paramount had hoped to close as early as July 22; a hearing on a longer preliminary injunction is set for August 3.
