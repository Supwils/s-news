### 1. Pokémon Pokopia tops 5 million sales; Nintendo shares close about 7% higher (Hit)

**Summary:** Nintendo and The Pokémon Company said Switch 2 exclusive Pokémon Pokopia has surpassed 5 million units sold worldwide, physical plus digital, just over four months after its March 5 launch. ## II. Regulation

### 6. UAE commercial gaming regulator signs MoU with ADGM’s financial watchdog (Regulation)

**Summary:** The UAE’s federal General Commercial Gaming Regulatory Authority said Friday it signed a memorandum of understanding with the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Abu Dhabi Global Market covering information sharing, supervisory coordination, and investigative assistance. GCGRA CEO Ciarán Carruthers said commercial gaming is one of the UAE’s newest regulated sectors and that credibility will be built through this kind of cooperation. ### 7. Philippines signs Anti-POGO implementing rules on criminalization and asset forfeiture (Regulation)

**Summary:** Philippine authorities signed the implementing rules and regulations for Republic Act No. 12312, the Anti-POGO Act, turning the 2024 ban on Philippine offshore gaming operations into an enforceable regime: the ban, criminalization of related activity, and forfeiture of assets used in prohibited operations. The Supreme Court separately adopted rules for civil forfeiture under Section 15, allowing buildings, facilities, gaming equipment, and proceeds to be forfeited to the state through expedited Regional Trial Court proceedings.

**Links:**

- [Abogado — Gov’t signs anti-POGO IRR; criminalization, asset forfeiture set](https://abogado.com.ph/usec-ty-govt-signs-anti-pogo-irr-criminalization-asset-forfeiture-set/)

**Commentary:** The ban is no longer a speech—it is a path to prosecutions and seizures, a harder close on Southeast Asian offshore gaming than licence debates.
