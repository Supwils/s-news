### 3. Nvidia Confirms Official DLSS 5 Path to RTX 40 GPUs—Still No Player Sliders (Tech)

## II. Industry Stress and Regulation

### 4. Epic CEO Tim Sweeney: Worst Industry Crash Since the 1980s (Industry)
**Summary:** Per Polygon’s reporting on Edge magazine interviews and related coverage, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney called the current downturn the worst video-game crash since the 1980s. He blamed AI data-center demand for bidding up RAM and storage (prices described as roughly quadrupling), pressuring console sales and lifting PS5, Xbox Series X, and Switch 2 prices amid shortages; he expects gaming-relevant hardware supply stress for about three years. AAA budgets were cited in the $250–400 million range, while Xbox’s ~3,200 layoffs and studio stoppages remain part of the backdrop.

**Links:**

### 5. India’s ED Raids Parimatch Network: 17 Sites, ~Rs 37 Crore Frozen (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per India Today on September 4, the Enforcement Directorate searched 17 locations across Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Delhi, Gujarat, and Uttar Pradesh in a money-laundering probe into Cyprus-linked betting platform Parimatch. Investigators said the network moved more than Rs 3,000 crore in a year; this sweep seized cash, gold, documents, and devices worth about Rs 2.11 crore and froze roughly Rs 37 crore in bank balances, bringing cumulative freezes/seizures to about Rs 150 crore. Proceeds were allegedly layered via mule accounts, travel operators, hawala, and crypto wallets.

**Links:**

- [India Today — ED raids Parimatch betting network across 5 states](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/parimatch-money-laundering-case-ed-raids-17-locations-freezes-rs-37-crore-2986735-2026-09-04)

**Commentary:** After the nationwide real-money gaming ban, enforcement is shifting from delisting apps to tracing payment chains.

### 6. Indian Tax Intelligence Flags ~INR 700bn Illegal Betting Flows, Wants Payment Traceability (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per iGaming EU’s September 4 summary of Economic Times reporting, India’s Directorate General of GST Intelligence identified about INR 700 billion (US$7.4 billion) in illegal online gaming/betting-related transactions in a single financial year—transaction volume, not confirmed operator revenue or tax loss. DGGI urged reforms so payment records name the originating website and disclose all bank accounts tied to a site’s GST registration, turning isolated transfers into a mappable network. Thousands of betting URLs have already been blocked, yet underground payments persist.

**Links:**

- [iGaming EU — India Traces $7.4bn in Illegal Online Betting Payments](https://theigaming.eu/2026/09/04/india-traces-7-4bn-in-illegal-online-betting-payments/)

**Commentary:** The post-ban battle is payment infrastructure—without website-level provenance, URL blocks just push traffic darker.
