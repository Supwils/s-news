## I. Platforms, Community, and Key Releases

### 1. Nexus Mods' Parent Acquires SteamDB: Community Data Hub Changes Hands, No Ads or Paywalls Promised for Now (Platform)
**Summary:** Per PC Gamer, Kotaku, Nexus Mods' official post, and SteamDB's FAQ on September 2, Chosen—the parent of Nexus Mods—has acquired third-party Steam database and player-tracking site SteamDB for an undisclosed sum. Founder Pavel "xPaw" Djundik said the project, started in 2013, had long been a one-person, donation-funded effort and that burnout and workload forced a handoff. Both sides say SteamDB keeps its own brand, URL, and community; existing free features will not move behind a paywall and display ads are not planned, though the operators acknowledge the need for sustainable revenue via affiliates and partnerships. xPaw will help with the transition for several months.

### 4. Italy's Lottomatica Agrees All-Share Absorption of Spain's Cirsa, Eyeing No. 2 Listed Gaming Spot (M&A)
**Summary:** Per Reuters, iGaming Business, and Il Sole 24 Ore on September 2, Milan-listed Lottomatica and Blackstone-backed Spanish operator Cirsa Enterprises signed a binding all-share cross-border merger-by-absorption; Cirsa holders receive 0.668 new Lottomatica shares per Cirsa share, with deal talk around €2.8 billion. Lottomatica survives as the combined entity, headquartered in Rome with a Barcelona branch; existing Lottomatica shareholders would own about 67.5%, Cirsa side about 32.5%, and Blackstone roughly 24% as the largest single investor. The companies cite about €2 billion pro forma adjusted EBITDA and roughly €115 million annual pre-tax cash synergies, targeting close in Q2 2027 pending shareholder and multi-jurisdiction gaming/competition approvals.
