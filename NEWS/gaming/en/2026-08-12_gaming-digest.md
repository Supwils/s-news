### 8. Malaysia's Federal Court upholds ruling that Kedah's lottery ban is unconstitutional (Regulation)

**Summary:** Malaysia's Federal Court on Aug 12 unanimously dismissed the Kedah state government's bid for leave to appeal a ruling that quashed its policy stopping issuance and renewal of business licenses for pool-betting outlets, ordering the state to pay RM50,000 in costs. The policy stemmed from a 2021 announcement to stop renewing lottery-outlet licenses from Jan 1, 2023; the High Court in 2024 found it ultra vires the Federal Constitution, and the Court of Appeal upheld that view. Counsel for betting operators said licensees can now reapply for premises licenses.
