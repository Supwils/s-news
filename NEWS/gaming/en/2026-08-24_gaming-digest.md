## IV. Regulation & Streaming

### 8. India Eyes PMLA Coverage for Real-Money Gaming; Madras HC Upholds Tamil Nadu Rules (Regulation)
**Summary:** Per Bharat Ki Awaaz on Aug 24 on two regulatory fronts: India's Finance Ministry is finalizing a proposal to designate real-money gaming platforms such as Dream11, Games24x7, and Winzo as "reporting entities" under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, subjecting them to bank-grade AML scrutiny, with inter-ministerial consultation underway; separately, the Madras High Court in Play Games 24×7 v. State of Tamil Nadu upheld Tamil Nadu's online-gambling ban and 2025 real-money gaming regulations, including Aadhaar-based KYC and a daily 12:00 a.m.–5:00 a.m. access ban, rejecting operators' overreach and constitutionality claims. Both moves follow the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 and 2026 rules now in force.
