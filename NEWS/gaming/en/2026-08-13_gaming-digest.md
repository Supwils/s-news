### 1. Amazon exits Western MMO ops as NCSoft takes TL in-house and Smilegate inherits Lost Ark (Retreat)

**Summary:** NCSoft said on Aug 13 it will bring global operations of MMORPG Throne and Liberty in-house by year-end, ending its Western publishing partnership with Amazon Game Studios to tighten the link between development and live ops. After the switch, Purple, Steam, PlayStation, and Xbox players are to sit in one global environment, with characters, progress, inventory, and Lucent preserved. Amazon separately said Western Lost Ark live ops will move to developer Smilegate in early 2027; Steam accounts and characters migrate by default, but mail, friend-list notes, and chat history will not, and players may opt out. With New World already set to shut on Jan 31, 2027, Amazon will no longer operate any MMO directly.
