### 7. India’s OGAI Presses App Stores and OTTs to Stop Enabling Real-Money Games (Regulation)

**Summary:** ThePrint and others detailed a July 29 Online Gaming Authority of India advisory to app stores, cloud providers, telcos, banks, social platforms, OTTs, and ad agencies to stop enabling, hosting, paying for, or advertising banned online money games under Sections 5–7 of the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (in force since May 1, 2026). Violations can bring up to three years’ imprisonment and fines up to Rs 1 crore; social games and esports remain permitted categories.

**Links:**

- [ThePrint — OGAI tells app stores, OTTs to stop enabling money games](https://theprint.in/india/governance/exclusive-1st-big-push-under-online-gaming-law-govt-tells-app-stores-otts-to-stop-enabling-money-games/3004996/)
- [IndianTelevision — MeitY warns platforms on online gaming law](https://indiantelevision.com/iworld/meity-warns-digital-platforms-to-comply-with-online-gaming-law/)

**Commentary:** After the statute landed, enforcement targets the payment–distribution–ads stack—real-money survival is decided at the store and bank API, not only in-game.

### 8. Austria Submits Gambling-Law Overhaul to EU Review (Regulation)

**Summary:** Austria’s Finance Ministry said on Aug 4 it formally notified the European Commission of a draft gambling-law overhaul, starting a three-month standstill while parliament can continue work. The draft centers on open online licensing, stronger player protection, a national self-exclusion register, and up to 13 casino licences—ending the current online-casino monopoly model.

**Links:**

- [European Gaming — Austria notifies EU of gambling law reform](https://europeangaming.eu/portal/latest-news/2026/08/04/210417/austria-gambling-law-reform-eu-notification/)

**Commentary:** Monopoly-to-licence reforms trade market access for deposit limits and exclusion tools—capital and grey markets will price the standstill carefully.
