### 8. Twelve States Sue to Block Paramount's $110B Warner Bros. Deal, Seek Temporary Restraining Order

**Summary:** On July 13, attorneys general from 12 states led by California's Rob Bonta filed suit in the Northern District of California to block Paramount Skydance's roughly $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. ### 9. Japan's LY Corp Discloses Leak of 7.1 Million Mobile Game User Identifiers

**Summary:** On July 14, Japanese IT company LY Corp, operator of the LINE messaging app, disclosed that internal identifiers for roughly 7.1 million users across three mobile games—LINE Pokopoko, LINE Pokopang Town, and discontinued LINE Pokopang—were mistakenly transmitted to an external advertising platform between May 2022 and April 3, 2026, due to a misconfigured ad analytics system. The company said the data consisted of random strings without names, addresses, or credit card numbers; the third party deleted the data and no secondary misuse has been confirmed. LY Corp identified the issue on April 1 and has completed fixes, apologizing to users the same day.

### 12. Gibraltar Prediction Market Regulations Take Effect July 13, First Dedicated Global Framework

**Summary:** NEXT.io reported on July 13 that Gibraltar's Prediction Market Regulations 2026 took effect under the Gambling Act 2025, creating a standalone regime stating prediction market activity "is not to be treated as betting, gaming or a lottery solely by reason of its characteristics as prediction market activity." Licensed operators may use stablecoins and other digital assets for settlement, while contracts involving death, crime, war, terrorism, and high insider-trading risk are prohibited. Authorities said two firms have received approval in principle, with a third expected within weeks. The move parallels U.S. CFTC debates over event-contract platforms.
