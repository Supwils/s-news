## II. Regulation & Regional News

### 7. New Zealand Online Casino Rules Take Effect July 3; 15-Licence EOI Opens July 17

**Summary:** Around July 5, The iGaming EU and MinterEllison analyzed New Zealand's Online Casino Gambling Regulations 2026, in force July 3, building on the Online Casino Gambling Act 2026 effective May 1 — the country's biggest gambling law overhaul in over 20 years. The Department of Internal Affairs (DIA) opens expression-of-interest (EOI) for 15 online casino licences on July 17; EOI fee is NZ$19,000 excl. GST. The three-stage process includes a September auction and October full applications. From December 1, providers without a licence application must stop serving New Zealand customers; violations carry fines up to NZ$5 million; fully licensed operations aren't expected until 2027.
