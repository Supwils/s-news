## IV. Regulation & M&A

### 8. EU Clears Saudi-Led ~$55B EA Buyout on Merger Rules; FSR Decision Window Nears (M&A)

**Summary:** The European Commission has approved under the EU Merger Regulation the ~$55 billion acquisition of Electronic Arts led by Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF) and partners, concluding the deal “would not raise competition concerns.” The transaction still faces review under the EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation (FSR), with multiple outlets previously citing a decision window around July 30, plus remaining clearances in the U.S. and elsewhere. If completed, it would be among history’s largest leveraged buyouts. GamesIndustry.biz, Engadget, and GamesRadar continue to track the process.

**Links:**

- [GamesIndustry.biz — EU Commission approves $55bn EA buyout](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/eu-commission-approves-55bn-ea-buyout-says-it-would-not-raise-competition-concerns)
