### 3. Xbox Disc-to-Digital opens to Insiders today: discs can claim digital entitlements (Platform)
**Summary:** Per Xbox Wire’s August 26 announcement and follow-on coverage, Xbox Insiders can begin testing Disc-to-Digital on August 31: insert a supported physical disc into an Xbox One or Series X optical console, launch the game, and claim a free account-tied digital entitlement that can unlock Play Anywhere and cloud gaming where supported, while the disc keeps working as before. Microsoft says the first wave covers “thousands” of One/Series disc titles and will grow; if the same disc is used on another console or profile, the digital entitlement may be revoked so each disc still carries one transferable license.

**Links:**

- [Xbox Wire — Your Discs. Now Also Digital.](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/08/26/your-discs-now-also-digital/)

**Commentary:** Before physical media fades further, Microsoft is translating shelf collections into account licenses rather than simply declaring the drive obsolete.
