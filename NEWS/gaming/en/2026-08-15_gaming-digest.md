**Summary:** Korean industry tallies on August 15 showed Krafton first-half revenue of 2.66 trillion won and operating profit of 972.5 billion won, up 73.3% and 38.3% year on year—about 80% of last year’s full-year 3.33 trillion won. ## IV. Regulation

### 8. Washington court curbs Kalshi sports and entertainment contracts as geofence deadlines near (Regulation)

**Summary:** King County Superior Court Judge John McHale entered injunction terms requiring prediction-market operator Kalshi to stop offering, in Washington, contracts on sports, elections, politics, entertainment, culture, tech and science, and “mentions.” Commodities, climate, economics, and finance contracts are outside the order. Kalshi must launch IP- and residency-based geofencing by August 19 and a GeoComply multi-source system by September 2; the attorney general’s office said missing the later deadline could bring penalties of up to $120,000 a day. Kalshi argues federal commodities law preempts state gambling rules; the Washington Court of Appeals denied a stay.
