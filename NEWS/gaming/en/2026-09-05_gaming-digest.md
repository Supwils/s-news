### 5. Lottomatica and Cirsa agree all-share merger aimed at No. 2 listed global operator (M&A)
**Summary:** Per iGaming Business and related coverage, Italy’s Lottomatica and Spain’s Cirsa agreed an all-share cross-border merger with Lottomatica as the surviving entity. Cirsa shareholders receive 0.668 new Lottomatica shares per Cirsa share; post-deal ownership is expected at about 67.5% for existing Lottomatica holders and 32.5% for Cirsa holders, with Blackstone becoming the largest shareholder (~24%). The combined group targets roughly €2 billion pro forma adjusted EBITDA and would rank as the second-largest listed gaming/sports-betting operator behind Flutter; about €115 million in annual pre-tax synergies are projected by year three. Closing is targeted for Q2 2027 pending shareholder and multi-jurisdiction regulatory approvals.
