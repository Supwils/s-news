### 7. Indian online gaming firms petition Supreme Court to review 28% GST full-face-value ruling (Regulation)

**Summary:** Around July 15, companies including Play Games24x7, Junglee Games, Sachiko Gaming and Head Digital Works (A23) filed Supreme Court review petitions against the May 27 judgment upholding a 28% GST on online gaming and fantasy sports involving pooled stakes. The ruling treated platforms as suppliers of actionable claims taxed on the full face value of bets/entries—not just platform fees—and treated 2023 amendments as clarificatory, enabling retrospective demands reported above Rs 1.5 lakh crore. The industry says dismissal would lock in a lasting rethink of unit economics and valuations.
