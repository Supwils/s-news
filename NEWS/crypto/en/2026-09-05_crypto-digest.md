## II. Regulation & Policy

### 3. Pakistan’s PVARA: existing VASPs must file NOCs by today or cease operations (Regulation)
**Summary:** The Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA) set Sep 5, 2026 as the deadline for existing virtual asset service providers to submit no-objection certificate (NOC) applications, warning that non-filers must stop operating; continuing without an application can be an offence. The licensing regime is live, and the regulator said Pakistan moved from primary law to open applications in under six months. An NOC is transitional clearance before a full licence; the State Bank has already allowed banks to open accounts for licensed VASPs and their customers. Today is the hard statutory cutoff.

### 5. MAS stablecoin legislative consultation underway, comments due Oct 16 (Regulation)
**Summary:** On Sep 1, Singapore’s Monetary Authority published a consultation on Payment Services Act 2019 amendments to implement its single-currency stablecoin (MAS-SCS) framework, with feedback due by Oct 16, 2026. Key themes include value stability, capital, redemption at par, and disclosure, plus proposals on multi-jurisdictional issuance, a ban on interest for MAS-regulated stablecoins, stress testing and orderly wind-down, and limited recognition of foreign stablecoins under comparable regimes. The first week of September also saw new rules take effect in Russia and Vietnam, tightening Asia’s compliance calendar.

**Links:**

- [MAS — Consults on Legislative Amendments to Implement Stablecoin Regulatory Framework](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/mas-consults-on-legislative-amendments-to-implement-stablecoin-regulatory-framework)
