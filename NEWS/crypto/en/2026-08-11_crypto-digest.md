## I. Regulation & Policy

### 1. SEC sets Aug. 14 open meeting to propose “Reg Crypto” token offering rules (Regulation)
**Summary:** On **August 11**, reports said the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission posted a Sunshine Act notice for an open meeting on **Friday, August 14**, at **10:00 a.m. ET** to consider proposing “Regulation Crypto”—a tailored offering regime for certain investment contracts involving crypto assets. The timing follows the Senate’s failure to advance the Digital Asset Market Clarity Act (CLARITY) before the August recess; TD Cowen analyst Jaret Seiberg called it the first of several SEC rulemakings after that setback. Approving publication would start a public comment period—typically about **two to three months**—not put the regime into force. Expected themes include a capital-raising path for some projects and an “off-ramp” from SEC jurisdiction once issuers no longer actively manage projects.
