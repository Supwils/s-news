### 8. Ethereum EIP-8363 tapered-issuance staking proposal draws fierce DeFi and staking backlash (DeFi)
**Summary:** On **Aug 7**, Cointelegraph Magazine and others detailed pushback against EIP-8363 (“Tapered Issuance Burn”), which would gradually cut staking rewards as more ETH is staked and drive new protocol issuance toward zero around a **50%** staked-supply threshold. ether.fi founder Mike Silagadze and others warned it could hit DeFi built on liquid staking; supporters argue it curbs excess issuance and concentration among custodians and liquid-staking providers. Critics also faulted publishing the proposal only about two days before the next-upgrade consideration deadline.
