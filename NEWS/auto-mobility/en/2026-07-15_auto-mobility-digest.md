### 3. NTSB: Tesla driver in fatal Texas house crash pressed accelerator to 100%, overriding FSD (Supervised) (Safety / US)
**Summary:** TechCrunch reported on July 15, 2026 that the National Transportation Safety Board confirmed Wednesday that in a June crash in Katy, Texas — in which a Tesla struck a house and killed 76-year-old resident Martha Avila — vehicle data showed the accelerator pedal pressed to 100%, overriding Full Self-Driving (Supervised). The car had been using FSD on a residential road with a 30 mph limit and was traveling over 70 mph at impact under clear, dry daytime conditions. Driver Michael Butler faces a manslaughter charge, and the victim's family has sued him and Tesla; the NTSB findings align with Tesla's post-crash account that the high-speed impact was not driven by FSD, while again highlighting human–machine control boundaries.

**Links:**

## II. China Market & Policy

### 4. CPCA: China passenger NEV retail down 8% YoY in first two weeks of July; penetration still 63.1% (Market / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost reported on July 15, 2026 that China Passenger Car Association data show passenger NEV retail at about 280,000 units for July 1–12, down 8% year on year and 3% from the same period a month earlier; NEV retail penetration still reached 63.1%, with wholesale penetration around 69.1%. Year-to-date passenger NEV retail totaled about 4.904 million units and remains under pressure; elevated channel inventories are dampening dealer restocking, with most brands controlling production to clear stock.

### 5. Ministries confirm 2027 vehicle-and-vessel tax changes: PHEVs and others lose exemptions; BEV passenger cars still untaxed (Policy / China)
**Summary:** China's Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration, and MIIT issued a July 2, 2026 notice (followed by People's Daily and MOF Q&A coverage): from January 1, 2027, half-rate vehicle-and-vessel tax for energy-saving cars ends, as does the exemption for pure-electric commercial vehicles, plug-in hybrids (including range extenders), and fuel-cell commercial vehicles — applying to both new and existing vehicles of those types. Official Q&A clarifies that pure-electric and fuel-cell passenger cars remain outside the tax base and are still not levied. Background cited includes 2025 NEV sales of about 16.49 million and domestic new-car NEV share above 50%, framing the shift as tax normalization after industry maturity.

**Links:**

- [People's Daily — Three ministries adjust energy-saving and NEV vehicle-and-vessel tax preferences](http://finance.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0704/c1004-40753239.html)
- [Ministry of Finance — Officials answer questions on vehicle-and-vessel tax preference adjustments](https://szs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcejiedu/202607/t20260703_3992830.htm)

**Commentary:** The rollback targets PHEVs and commercial NEVs while keeping BEV passenger cars outside the tax — a fine-tuning of powertrain incentives rather than a full withdrawal of support.
