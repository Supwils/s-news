## I. EVs & Global Markets

### 1. IEA: Global EV sales jump 35% QoQ in Q2; 2026 share outlook raised to 29% (Markets / Policy)
**Summary:** On July 30, the International Energy Agency released *Electric Car Markets in a Time of Uncertainty*. Spurred by the Middle East energy crisis and higher fuel prices, global EV sales rose about 35% in Q2 2026 versus Q1, with quarterly records in 50 countries. Overall car sales fell roughly 5% year-on-year in H1, but the EV rebound largely offset the weak first quarter. The IEA raised its 2026 EV share of global car sales to 29% from 28% in the May outlook (about 23 million units). Markets including Australia, Brazil, India, Korea, and Viet Nam roughly doubled March–June sales versus 2025. China and the U.S. remain soft; China’s full-year EV volume outlook was revised to roughly match 2025 (about 13.2 million), though share may exceed 60%.

**Commentary:** Fuel-price shocks again prove energy security is a hard driver of electrification, while weak China–U.S. demand still caps the global growth ceiling.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Regulation

### 5. Amazon’s Zoox wins NHTSA commercial exemption for paid, steering-wheel-free robotaxis (Autonomy)
**Summary:** On July 30, the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration granted Amazon-owned Zoox a temporary exemption to commercially charge for rides in purpose-built robotaxis without traditional steering wheels or pedals—described as the first U.S. commercial deployment exemption for such vehicles. The two-year grant allows up to about 2,500 vehicles per year under enhanced, adaptable federal oversight and reporting. Zoox said paid service will start next month in Las Vegas, then expand as state rules allow; California still needs DMV and PUC commercialization permits.

**Links:**

- [CNBC — Amazon's Zoox to begin charging for rides in Las Vegas](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/amazon-zoox-robotaxi-rides-las-vegas.html)
- [TechCrunch — Zoox clears final federal hurdle for paid robotaxi](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/zoox-clears-final-federal-hurdle-to-launch-paid-robotaxi-service/)

**Commentary:** With the federal gate open, rivalry shifts to state permits, incident response, and real unit economics.

### 6. Same day, NHTSA advances AV standards work and exemption reforms with a $5M three-year consortium (Policy)
**Summary:** Alongside the Zoox grant, NHTSA announced a package to speed automated-vehicle deployment: a three-year, about $5 million A2SCEND consortium with SAE Industry Technologies Consortia to gather data for first-ever federal AV performance standards; an interim final rule letting vehicles built before an exemption still qualify for commercial deployment; a streamlined Part 555 exemption process; and the first update since 2017 to AV safety guidance, seeking comment on emergency response, remote assistance, and post-crash behavior.

**Links:**

### 10. Kumamoto quake ripples through Japan’s auto supply chain: Toyota, Nissan halt lines; Aisin plant damaged (Supply chain)
**Summary:** After a roughly magnitude-7 quake near Kumamoto on July 28, disruptions continued through July 30.
