### 1. CPCA projects ~1.04M August NEV retail sales and 65.8% penetration as Chengdu show closes (Market / China)
**Summary:** The China Passenger Car Association’s August outlook points to roughly 1.58 million narrow passenger-car retail sales (−21.7% YoY, +8.1% MoM), including about 1.04 million NEV retail units and a record ~65.8% retail penetration—nearly two electrified vehicles for every three sold. ### 2. Europe’s July BEV sales jump ~51%; Chinese brands hit a record ~11.2% share (Market / Europe)
**Summary:** Preliminary Dataforce figures show July new-car registrations across the EU, UK, Norway, Switzerland and Iceland up about 4.1% YoY. Fully electric sales rose ~51% YoY to roughly 25% of the European new-car market, with year-to-date BEV growth near 37%. Chinese brands’ combined share reached a record ~11.2% as volumes roughly doubled (+107% YoY); BYD, Chery and SAIC (including MG) led, while Leapmotor and XPeng posted even steeper gains. Tesla’s European sales fell ~36% YoY. Strong Renault 5 and Twingo results underscore that the fight is shifting toward affordable EVs as Chinese brands expand.

## III. Safety & Recalls

### 4. Lucid recalls 27,185 Air sedans over exterior-lighting fire risk; OTA already covers most (Recall / US)
**Summary:** CNBC, citing NHTSA, said Lucid is recalling about 27,185 model-year 2022–2026 Air luxury sedans in the US because an exterior lighting circuit may lack sufficient overcurrent protection, risking overheating and fire as well as loss of exterior lights that could raise crash risk. NHTSA advised owners to park outdoors and away from structures until the remedy is applied. Lucid’s fix is an over-the-air software update (about version 2.10.0 or later); roughly 20,719 vehicles had already received it. It is Lucid’s largest recall to date—larger than its ~15,841 global deliveries in 2025.

**Links:**

- [CNBC — Lucid to recall more than 27,000 Air sedans over fire risk](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/ev-maker-lucid-to-recall-more-than-27000-luxury-sedans-over-fire-risk-nhtsa-says.html)
- [CarBuzz — Lucid Air fire recall details](https://carbuzz.com/lucid-air-sedan-fire-recall-august-2026/)

**Commentary:** Luxury EV trust is fragile—“park outside until further notice” lands harder than any range claim.

## V. Charging Infrastructure

### 10. BYD flash-charging network hits 10,000 stations: doubled in under five months, 20,000 targeted by year-end (Charging / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost and The Next Web reported that BYD marked its 10,000th China flash-charging station on Aug 28 at a Shenzhen Longhua flagship site—halfway to a 20,000-station 2026 year-end goal. The network roughly doubled from ~5,000 stations on April 1 in under five months. Cumulative delivery is about 210 million kWh to more than 1.83 million users, nearly one-third of them non-BYD drivers. Peak output reaches ~1,500 kW per connector; with second-generation Blade batteries, BYD claims 10%–97% in about nine minutes. Hitting 20,000 would require ~80 new stations per day; 18,000 are planned with partners and 2,000 on highways, with ~300 UK sites targeted this year.
