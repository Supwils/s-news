## I. EVs & Markets

### 1. Tesla’s China retail softens while Shanghai exports surge — plant tilts toward an export hub (Market / Tesla)
**Summary:** Electrek reported on Aug 4, citing China Passenger Car Association data, that Tesla’s China domestic retail sales in H1 2026 fell about 19% from the 2023 peak to a multi-year low, while exports from Giga Shanghai jumped about 127% to roughly 228,994 vehicles (from about 101,064 in H1 2025). Separately, Reuters said Shanghai-built Model 3/Y wholesale volume in July—including exports—reached 93,579 units, up 37.8% year over year and about 5% month over month, the ninth straight growth month. Intensifying local competition and aging core models are pushing more Shanghai output toward Europe, Canada, and other Asian markets.

## II. Policy & Charging Infrastructure

### 5. China’s 15th Five-Year power-system plan: 40M+ chargers by 2030 to support 110M+ EVs (Policy / Charging)
**Summary:** People’s Daily reported on Aug 4 that the NDRC and National Energy Administration issued the New-Type Power System Construction plan for the 15th Five-Year period, targeting more than 40 million charging points by 2030—including about 300,000 high-power units—and service capacity for more than 110 million electric vehicles. The plan calls for city-area, highway-corridor, and rural-point networks, higher fast-charge share, highway service-area upgrades, and high-power truck charging along major freight routes. The NEA earlier said China already had about 23.057 million charging points by end-June 2026.

**Summary:** Electrek and others reported on Aug 4 that San Francisco startup Revoy closed about $27 million in Series A funding led by Standard Capital to scale a lower-cost second-generation detachable electric dolly.
