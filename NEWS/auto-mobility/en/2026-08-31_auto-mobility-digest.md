## I. OEM Collaboration & Software-Defined Vehicles

### 1. Honda and Nissan Sign Next-Gen SDV Joint Development Pact: Shared Core ECUs and In-Vehicle OS (Software / Japan)
**Summary:** On Aug 31, Honda and Nissan announced a joint development agreement to standardize core electronic control units for next-generation software-defined vehicles (SDVs)—including high-performance main ECUs and zone ECUs—plus the in-vehicle operating system, key middleware, and vehicle control software. Both companies plan to apply the resulting E/E architecture in their next-generation SDVs from fiscal year 2029. The deal aims to shorten development cycles, cut R&D costs, and unlock scale; it is the most concrete software tie-up since their 2025 merger talks collapsed.

**Links:**

**Commentary:** After a failed merger, “soft integration” without equity: sharing the vehicle brain to match Chinese and U.S. SDV spending intensity.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Robotaxis

### 2. DiDi Starts Fully Driverless R2 Robotaxi Trials: Bookable in Beijing and Guangzhou Demo Zones (AV / China)
**Summary:** On Aug 31, DiDi Autonomous Driving said its next-generation Robotaxi R2—jointly developed with GAC Aion—has begun fully driverless passenger-service trials in selected demonstration areas in Beijing and Guangzhou, bookable via the DiDi app. R2 upgrades driving performance and cabin experience; since delivery in January 2026 it has been tested on public roads in Guangzhou, Beijing, Shenzhen and other cities. DiDi says its full-stack Level 4 stack and Huiju Port operations center have supported more than 2,200 consecutive days of safe operations.

### 3. Pony.ai and FutureLink Plan 200 Robotaxis in Korea: First 10 Units Due Next Month (AV / South Korea)
**Summary:** Electronic Times reported on Aug 31 that Pony.ai and FutureLink—an AV unit of Kosdaq-listed PonyLink, in which Pony.ai holds a stake—will bring seventh-generation robotaxis based on BAIC Arcfox Alpha T5 into South Korea, targeting 200 vehicles. The seventh-gen design adds braking, steering and power redundancy and is said to cut AV kit cost by about 70% versus the prior generation.
