## I. EVs & Policy

### 1. CAAM: July NEV monthly share tops 60% for first time; cumulative share crosses 50% (Market / China)
**Summary:** On Aug 12, the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers released July 2026 production and sales data. Total auto output and sales were 2.573 million and 2.584 million units, down 0.7% and 0.3% year over year; CAAM Deputy Secretary-General Chen Shihua attributed the dip to the traditional off-season, front-loaded H1 demand, heat, and localized flood impacts. NEV production and sales reached 1.576 million and 1.561 million units, up 26.8% and 23.7%; BEV output and sales each exceeded 1 million units with growth above 30%. NEVs accounted for 60.4% of new-car sales in July—the first monthly breach of 60%—while the Jan–Jul cumulative share also crossed 50% for the first time. Jan–Jul NEV output and sales totaled 9.014 million and 9.007 million units (about +10% YoY). July auto exports hit 1.043 million units (+81.3%), including 553,000 NEV exports (+150%), marking a second straight month above 1 million exports with NEVs above half of export volume.

### 2. California MyFirstEV: Tesla exhausts its quota in five days; CARB chair cites 40% sales jump (Policy / US)
**Summary:** California's June budget created the MyFirstEV program, offering first-time ZEV buyers up to $3,500 on new cars and $1,750 on used ones, with automakers matching state funds 1:1. The $135.5 million state pool is split among roughly 13–15 brands at about $9 million each. Tesla went live Aug 3 and exhausted its entire allocation by Aug 8—an estimated $18 million in combined state and OEM-matched rebates in five days. On Aug 12, CARB Chair Lauren Sanchez told POLITICO's California Agenda: Sacramento Summit that one automaker had already run out of funding and that weekly sales jumped about 40% after launch; CARB confirmed the automaker was Tesla. Hyundai and Lucid still have balances; Ford, Rivian, and others join in Aug–Sep.

**Summary:** The Korea Metal Workers' Union Hyundai branch began a new round of partial strikes Aug 12: four-hour walkouts per shift on Aug 12–13, rising to six hours on Aug 14 and 18, with overtime and special shifts refused through Aug 18. ### 10. Sinopec converts Shanghai gas station to BYD 1,500 kW flash-charging hub (Charging / China)
**Summary:** Electrek reported Aug 11 that Sinopec removed underground tanks and fuel pumps at a Shanghai station, converting the site into an EV-only flash-charging hub with BYD chargers rated up to 1,500 kW.
