### 3. Honda ends Prologue in North America, leaving a mainstream BEV gap (Strategy / North America)
**Summary:** CleanTechnica and others reported on Jul 21 that Honda will discontinue the Prologue, its only mainstream battery-electric passenger model in North America, with dealer inventory expected to clear into early 2027. After that, Honda’s North American passenger lineup will temporarily lack a mainstream BEV, leaving only the low-volume CR-V e fuel-cell model (mainly California leases). The move follows Honda’s March 2026 decision to abandon the 0 Series EV program and take about a $15.5 billion impairment, shifting focus back to hybrids.

## IV. Pricing Trends & Charging Infrastructure

### 9. ICCT/Fraunhofer: comparable BEV real prices in Germany fell ~18% over five years (Pricing / Europe)
**Summary:** On Jul 22, research from the ICCT and Fraunhofer ISI found that after adjusting for inflation and attributes such as weight, range, and power, comparable battery-electric vehicles in Germany became about 18% cheaper from 2020 to 2025, while real ICE prices rose about 2%. Global battery pack prices fell about 21%–24% nominally over the period (from roughly €165–185/kWh to about €130–140/kWh), or about 35%–37% after inflation. Available BEV models in Germany rose from 38 in 2020 to 159 in 2025. Researchers stress that stable CO₂ fleet targets remain critical for investment and cost parity.

**Links:**

- [eeNews Europe — Battery EV prices fall 18% as model choice quadruples](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/battery-ev-prices-fall-18-as-model-choice-quadruples/)

**Commentary:** Rising sticker prices are often misread as “EVs got pricier”—comparable real-price declines are what actually fuels adoption.
