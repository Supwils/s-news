**Summary:** Automotive News and electrive reported on July 2, 2026, that VW subsidiary Cariad and Bosch confirmed termination of the 2022 Automated Driving Alliance (ADA). ### 12. US Declines USMCA Renewal; North American Auto Trade Enters Annual Review Cycle Through 2036 (Policy / North America)
**Summary:** Car and Driver and Supply Chain Intelligence Brief reported July 1–2, 2026, that the Trump administration declined to renew the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) on schedule — the pact remains in force but will not auto-extend to 2042, instead entering annual reviews until its 2036 sunset; any party may exit with six months' written notice. Washington seeks to raise automotive rules of origin from 75% to 82% and require 50% US content. Canada and Mexico had sought renewal; the next negotiation round is July 20 in Mexico City, with Canada excluded from this bilateral round. North American auto supply-chain trade flows exceed ~$1.8 trillion annually.
