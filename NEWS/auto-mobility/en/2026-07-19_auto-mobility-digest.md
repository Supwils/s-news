## I. Battery Safety & Quality

### 1. GAC Aion AION S fleet battery faults: warranty extended to 8 years / 300,000 km; no mass recall yet (Battery / China)
**Summary:** Per a Jul 19 report by Cailian Press (via Sina Finance), as two mandatory EV standards (GB 38031-2025 and GB 18384-2025) took effect on Jul 1, some AION S commercial fleets using CALB (Zhongchuangxinhang) 177Ah LFP cells drew clustered complaints of cell swelling, leakage, and insulation faults. GAC Aion said on the evening of Jul 18 that a preliminary probe found battery faults on some commercial-use AION S vehicles with that cell; it extended battery warranty from 8 years / 150,000 km to 8 years / 300,000 km, pledged free inspection/repair or pack replacement for anomalies, and said operating subsidies will apply when repairs exceed five days. CALB said the same day it had started maintenance via Aion dealers and would offer direct service; neither side confirmed a mass recall or publicly settled liability.

- [Xinhua — New power-battery national standards raise the safety bar](http://www.news.cn/finance/20260714/8cbc0c365ef24d9fa3344f4cc8625ab4/c.html)

**Commentary:** New rules harden entry for new models, but in-service commercial packs still lean on aftersales patches—whether extended warranty and monitoring can substitute for a recall is the next test.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Robotaxis

### 2. Amazon’s Zoox recalls software on 105 robotaxis after a smoke-obscured fire scene in Las Vegas (Autonomy / US)
**Summary:** CNBC and NHTSA filings show Zoox filed a voluntary software recall (26E044) on Jul 8 covering all 105 robotaxis operating on public roads. On Jun 20 in Las Vegas, an unoccupied vehicle entered an active emergency fire scene obscured by heavy smoke and not yet coned off, braked hard, then reversed under remote guidance; no injuries were reported. The update strengthens smoke and emergency-scene detection; Zoox said the patch rolled out around Jul 15. The recall follows NHTSA’s call for AV developers to fix first-responder interference by month-end, after Waymo recalled about 3,900 vehicles last month for entering closed freeway work zones.

**Links:**

- [CNBC — Amazon's Zoox recalls software after robotaxi drove into heavy smoke](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/17/amazon-zoox-recalls-robotaxi-smoke.html)
- [NHTSA — Recall acknowledgment 26E044 (Zoox ADS software)](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2026/RCAK-26E044-8851.pdf)

**Commentary:** Emergency scenes are not edge cases—regulators are making smoke, cones, and warning lights a hard gate for commercial robotaxi scale.

## III. Automaker Strategy & Markets

### 4. Honda confirms Prologue EV SUV ends after the 2026 model year, leaving no Honda BEV in the US for now (OEM / US)
**Summary:** Jalopnik, CarBuzz, and Car and Driver reported in mid-to-late July that Honda will conclude Prologue sales after completing the 2026 model year, while continuing dealer service, parts, and warranty support; remaining inventory may sell into early 2027. The GM Ultium-based crossover, built in Mexico and on sale since March 2024, managed only about 8,407 US sales in H1 2026, down roughly 48.5% year over year. With Honda’s 0 Series and Acura RSX EV plans already canceled and the Acura ZDX previously discontinued, Honda will temporarily have no battery-electric passenger car on sale in the United States.
