### 2. California AB 1777 takes effect July 1: traffic violations can be issued to AV manufacturers (Policy / US)
**Summary:** GMToday reported in July 2026 that statewide autonomous-vehicle rules based on Assembly Bill 1777 took effect on July 1, 2026, allowing California law enforcement to issue "notices of AV noncompliance" to manufacturers when driverless vehicles violate traffic laws, replacing a framework that could only cite human drivers. After a notice is filed, the DMV investigates and requires remediation; repeated or uncorrected violations can lead to restricted or suspended operating permits. New rules also require 50,000 or 500,000 miles of testing (depending on vehicle weight) before deployment, annual first-responder interaction plan updates, and two-minute fleet withdrawal after emergency geofencing directives, while opening pathways for certain heavy-duty autonomous freight and transit pilots.

### 3. US Congress weighs national EV registration fee under BUILD America 250 Act, starting at $130/year (Policy / US)
**Summary:** Marketplace reported on July 7, 2026 that the bipartisan BUILD America 250 Act would create the first nationwide registration fee for electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles to offset declining federal gas-tax revenue for the Highway Trust Fund. Under the House version, EV owners would pay $130 annually and plug-in hybrids $35 starting in fiscal 2027, rising to caps of $150 and $50 respectively from 2029; the CBO estimates roughly $12 billion in net revenue over ten years, a small fraction of the trust fund shortfall. With the federal gas tax unchanged since 1993 and 42 states already charging local EV fees, Brookings scholars warn the lump-sum annual charge could dampen purchase intent alongside the expired federal EV purchase tax credit.

**Links:**

- [Marketplace — Congress eyes a national EV registration fee](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2026/07/07/congress-eyes-a-national-ev-registration-fee)
- [Bond Buyer — Despite user fees, surface transportation bill deepens Highway Trust Fund deficit: CBO](https://www.bondbuyer.com/news/despite-user-fees-surface-transportation-bill-deepens-highway-trust-fund-deficit-cbo)

**Commentary:** After purchase incentives fade, US EV policy is shifting from subsidies to usage fees — a national registration charge would rewrite fifty years of fuel-tax-only federal road funding.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Mobility

### 4. NHTSA reviews dropping steering-wheel mandate for robotaxis; administrator cites "never human-driven" rationale (Regulation / US)
**Summary:** Briefs reported on July 11, 2026 that NHTSA Administrator Jonathan Morrison said the agency is evaluating whether to remove the steering-wheel requirement for fully autonomous vehicles, arguing that vehicles designed never to be human-operated should not need manual controls. The review follows NHTSA's June 2026 update eliminating the foot-brake mandate for driverless cars, part of the Trump administration's push to modernize AV rules and potentially clear design obstacles for Tesla's Cybercab and Zoox's purpose-built robotaxis. Morrison provided no timeline; safety advocates remain concerned about emergency scenarios lacking human override.
