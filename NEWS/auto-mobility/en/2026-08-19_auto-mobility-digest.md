## I. EVs and Policy

### 1. U.S. EPA rescinds the 2009 GHG Endangerment Finding and rolls back LD/MD/HD vehicle greenhouse-gas standards (Policy / United States)
**Summary:** On February 12, 2026, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) completed its rescission of the 2009 “greenhouse gas endangerment finding” and concluded that, under the Clean Air Act, it no longer has the statutory authority to set new vehicle greenhouse-gas emission standards. EPA also repealed all subsequent greenhouse-gas emission standards for light-, medium-, and heavy-duty vehicles (and the engines) covered by those rules. EPA said the move is the single largest deregulatory action in U.S. history and expects to save “over $1.3 trillion.”
**Links:**

- [US EPA — Final Rule: Rescission of the Greenhouse Gas Endangerment Finding and Motor Vehicle Greenhouse Gas Emission Standards Under the Clean Air Act](https://www.epa.gov/regulations-emissions-vehicles-and-engines/final-rule-rescission-greenhouse-gas-endangerment)
- [Federal Register — Rescission of the Greenhouse Gas Endangerment Finding and Motor Vehicle GHG Standards (Vol. 91, No. 32)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-18/pdf/2026-03157.pdf)

**Commentary:** When the regulatory basis for GHG standards disappears, investment plans across automakers and suppliers can shift immediately from emissions targets to alternative priorities.

### 2. “National Consumer Subsidy” and purchase-tax cuts stack in 2026: up to 35,000 yuan in savings for eligible NEV buyers (Policy / China)
**Summary:** Xinhua said that after the 2026 consumption-subsidy program takes effect, buyers who take the scrappage route and purchase eligible NEV passenger cars can receive a subsidy at 12% of the new vehicle price, capped at 20,000 yuan. For the trade-in route, the subsidy is 8% of the new vehicle price, capped at 15,000 yuan. The report also said the purchase tax continues as “half collection,” with a per-vehicle cap of 15,000 yuan, and that the subsidy and purchase-tax cut can be combined for up to 35,000 yuan in total savings. It also noted that eligibility depends on updated technical requirements, including raising plug-in (including range-extended) pure-electric range thresholds from 43 km to 100 km.
**Links:**

- [Xinhua — National subsidy lands: how much can you save on buying a NEV](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260112/4fd3275488cd4d49afaa9252dc8d77aa/c.html)

**Commentary:** Stacking benefits with tightened eligibility effectively shortens the decision window for consumers, and pulls demand forward.

## II. Autonomous Driving and Mobility Platforms

### 4. Nevada approves Tesla's Vegas Robotaxi with only 10 vehicles: 45 mph cap and airport-trip bans (Autonomous driving / United States)
**Summary:** Multiple reports say Nevada regulators approved Tesla's Robotaxi permit with an initial fleet of 10 vehicles, down from the company's request of 5,000. The permit confines operations to an approved geofenced area on the Strip, caps speed at 45 mph, and restricts airport-related pickups and drop-offs (including a ban on airport trips without extra approval). The approval also requires “appropriate human supervision” and includes reporting obligations for incidents.

### 5. Seoul doubles late-night autonomous taxi fleet in Gangnam: 19 vehicles from seven, expanding road coverage (Autonomous driving / South Korea)
**Summary:** The Korea Times reported that Seoul plans to expand the late-night autonomous taxi service in Gangnam District. Starting Thursday at 10 p.m., the fleet will rise to 19 vehicles from seven. The expansion also extends autonomous driving to all roads within the operating zone, including child-protection areas, after receiving approval from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport. The city said the service has completed 15,140 rides since it began on Sept. 26, 2024, and fares are differentiated by late-night time bands.
