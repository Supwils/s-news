## I. Policy & Regulation

### 1. China to end vehicle and vessel tax breaks for PHEVs, range-extenders, and BEV commercial vehicles from 2027 (Policy / China)
**Summary:** CnEVPost, Global Times, and Beijing News reported on July 3, 2026, that China's Ministry of Finance, State Taxation Administration, and MIIT jointly announced that from January 1, 2027, tax halving for energy-saving ICE vehicles and full exemptions for BEV commercial vehicles, plug-in hybrids (including range-extenders), and fuel-cell commercial vehicles will be canceled. BEV and fuel-cell passenger cars remain outside the displacement-based vehicle and vessel tax scope and stay effectively untaxed. PHEV owners will pay annual taxes of roughly 360–660 yuan ($50–$90) depending on province. Vehicles purchased before the cutoff lose eligibility retroactively. Officials cited record NEV penetration (62.9% retail share in May) as the industry matures beyond broad tax support.

### 2. China's EV safety "dual standards" take effect July 1 for newly homologated models (Safety / China)
**Summary:** Sina Finance, Tencent News, and China's national standards platform confirm that GB 18384-2025 (Electric Vehicle Safety Requirements) and GB 38031-2025 (Power Battery Safety Requirements for Electric Vehicles) became mandatory on July 1, 2026. New rules require physical (not software-only) emergency disconnect, no fire or explosion within a 2-hour thermal-runaway observation window with monitoring points below 60°C, plus new mandatory bottom-impact and post-fast-charging safety tests. New type-approval applications must comply immediately; legacy approved models get a 1-year transition for GB 18384 (until July 1, 2027) and a 13-month delay for GB 38031. Already-sold vehicles are unaffected.

**Links:**

- [Sina Finance — Two new mandatory EV safety national standards take effect July 1](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-06-25/doc-inieqsme2581798.shtml)
- [National Standards Platform — GB 38031-2025 Power Battery Safety Requirements](https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=31D72E7FB9BF92EDE06397BE0A0AC2E0)

**Commentary:** "No fire, no explosion" is now codified — China's NEV race is upgrading from price wars to safety compliance, with fast-charging battery validation as the new frontier.
