## II. Autonomous Driving & Mobility

### 4. NHTSA Pedal-Free Robotaxi Brake Rule Comments Close July 27 (Policy / U.S.)
**Summary:** Coverage dated July 26 notes that NHTSA’s proposed update to FMVSS No. 135 would remove the foot-operated brake-pedal requirement for vehicles designed exclusively for Automated Driving System operation, while keeping stopping-distance performance rules intact; the public comment window closes July 27, 2026. If finalized, the change would create a clearer federal certification path for pedal-free, steering-wheel-free robotaxis and weaken reliance on exemption regimes capped at about 2,500 vehicles per manufacturer per year — a material issue for Tesla’s Cybercab and Amazon’s Zoox.

**Links:**
