### 6. GAC Aion S battery crisis: CALB cells blamed; ~213,000 vehicles under pressure (Battery / Quality)
**Summary:** CarNewsChina reported on Jul 21 that GAC Aion S owners have logged swelling, leakage, and cell ruptures, including power loss while driving, concentrated in cars with about 150,000–300,000 km. Complaint platforms logged about 182 cases in two weeks, with a ~4.7% fault detection rate across roughly 213,000 affected vehicles, traced to CALB 177Ah LFP cells. After Xinhua scrutiny, Aion and CALB apologized on Jul 18 and offered extended battery warranties (8 years / 300,000 km), free inspection and replacement, and repair subsidies—opting for warranty remediation rather than a formal recall.

**Links:**

- [CarNewsChina — CALB battery issues trigger massive failures in GAC Aion S fleet](https://carnewschina.com/2026/07/21/calb-battery-issues-trigger-massice-failures-in-gac-aion-s-fleet/)
