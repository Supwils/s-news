## I. Policy & China Market

### 1. Four Chinese ministries launch a one-year vehicle production-conformity and quality campaign (Policy / China)
**Summary:** Xinhua, China Economic Net, and CnEVPost reported on Aug 27 that MIIT, the Ministry of Public Security, the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, and SAMR issued a notice launching a nationwide, one-year special campaign to raise production conformity and quality for road motor vehicles. The drive targets four problem areas: production conformity, reliability, durability, and validation of new technologies. Focus items include whether manufacturing processes ensure reliability, new-vehicle design life and R&D cycles, defects such as corrosion and suspension failures, and whether combined driver-assistance and autonomous-driving functions—and vehicle/cloud data and cybersecurity—are adequately tested. Regulators also pledged to crack down on exaggerated marketing and intensify spot checks on models linked to irrational competition. The move elevates earlier point inspections of automakers and labs into a cross-agency national campaign, against a backdrop of large door-handle recalls and public concern over aggressive designs installed without full validation.

### 2. MIIT: 15th Five-Year Plan push for EV charging stations in every county and piles in every township (Charging / Policy)
**Summary:** Sina Finance reported on Aug 27 that MIIT Vice Minister Xin Guobin said at a State Council Information Office briefing on Aug 26 that during the 15th Five-Year Plan period China will push for new-energy-vehicle charging stations in every county and charging piles in every township, alongside trade-in programs, NEV rural rollout, and county-level charging/swap gap-filling pilots. He also flagged persistent irrational competition, cases of aggressive designs installed without adequate validation, and isolated quality and autonomous-driving safety incidents that drew public attention. Officials are drafting a 15th Five-Year Plan for the intelligent connected NEV industry, supporting battery cold-weather durability and AD safety R&D, and barring inadequately tested products from market entry.

## II. Autonomous Driving & Robotaxi

### 3. XPeng’s second-gen VLA: Robotaxi wins Guangzhou remote-test permit, enters front-seat-empty public-road validation (AD / China)
**Summary:** Phoenix Tech and Sina Finance reported on Aug 27 that at its Physical AI and second-generation VLA event, XPeng said Robotaxis running the second-gen VLA recently received Guangzhou intelligent connected vehicle remote-testing credentials. The vehicles may run on designated Level 1–3 test roads in Guangzhou with no safety driver in the front seat, under cloud remote monitoring and takeover—marking entry into critical driverless public-road validation. Employee internal trials since June have completed more than 2,000 full trip loops. XPeng said the second-gen VLA uses one technical base spanning L2 to L4, supporting Robotaxi upward and distilling to production cars downward. The new version adds Infini-VLA long-horizon memory (about the prior 30 seconds), an X-Foresight predictive world model (about a 6-second look-ahead), claimed roughly 300% faster end-to-end response, and a distilled Turing VLA 2.0 Lite aimed at more production models and existing owners.

### 5. BYD unveils second pickup in Brazil: mid-size Mako PHEV on Song Plus platform, on sale in September (Product / Overseas)
**Summary:** CarNewsChina reported on Aug 27 that BYD officially unveiled its second pickup in Brazil, the Mako (Portuguese anequim, mako shark), a mid-size model complementing the Shark. About 4.8 meters long and based on the Song Plus platform, it uses fifth-generation DM: a 1.5L naturally aspirated engine (about 73 kW) plus motor for combined system output of about 166 kW and about 400 N·m, with a roughly 12.9 kWh pack. The initial version is FWD with DHT; a rear-motor electric AWD variant is expected, and the powertrain is ethanol-compatible for Brazil’s fuel mix. Pricing is expected around 200,000–220,000 Brazilian reais (about USD 39,700–44,100), versus rivals such as the Fiat Toro. Launch is planned for September 2026 in Brazil, starting with China imports before shifting to the Camaçari plant in Bahia alongside Song Pro and Dolphin. BYD says it aims for five pickup models in its global matrix.
