## I. EVs & Policy

### 1. California’s MyFirstEV launches: $3,500 instant rebate for first-time ZEV buyers (Policy / US)
**Summary:** California Gov. Gavin Newsom announced the MyFirstEV rollout, offering first-time zero-emission vehicle buyers or lessees an instant $3,500 discount on new ZEVs (generally priced up to $50,000) and $1,750 off certified used ZEVs (generally up to $25,000). The state is putting in about $135.5 million, matched dollar-for-dollar by participating automakers for a combined pool near $270 million, administered by CARB on a first-come, first-served basis. Hyundai/Genesis, Lucid, and Tesla went live first, with Ford, Rivian, Chevrolet, and Kia expected later in August and Toyota/Honda among brands targeted for September.

- [CBS San Francisco — Newsom announces rollout of California's $3,500 EV rebate](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/california-ev-rebate-myfirstev-electric-vehicles/)

**Commentary:** With the federal purchase tax credit gone, state-level point-of-sale rebates are becoming a key demand backstop for U.S. electrification.

### 2. Oversized heavy EVs strain China’s roads as fuel-tax revenue shrinks (Policy / China)
**Summary:** The Straits Times reports that about six in 10 new models launched in the first half of 2026 stretched beyond 5 meters, while compact models under 4.5 meters fell to roughly 2% of launches. Heavier EVs accelerate road wear even as fuel-tax receipts that fund maintenance decline with rising EV penetration, squeezing local highway budgets. Beijing has already cut the long-running NEV purchase-tax break to 5% with a 15,000-yuan cap, and plans to end annual vehicle-and-vessel tax exemptions for plug-in hybrids and extended-range EVs; mileage-based road charges are under discussion.

**Links:**

- [The Straits Times — Giant EVs are damaging China’s roads without paying for repairs](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/motoring/giant-evs-are-damaging-chinas-roads-without-paying-for-repairs)

**Commentary:** After the electrification boom, road-use costs and fiscal sustainability are moving to the center of China’s next policy correction.

## II. Markets & Regions

### 4. Europe’s BEV surge widens a north–south divide; Denmark’s private buyers near 97% in July (Market / Europe)
**Summary:** Mid-year European data show battery-electric registrations outpacing the broader new-car market, with June BEV share above about 25% of new cars. Denmark’s H1 BEV share reached roughly 79.9%, and July private-buyer BEV share was reported near 97%; Finland and Sweden also remain highly electrified. Italy and Spain still sit below about 10% BEV share, and Poland around 5.3%, underscoring a structural split between mature Nordic markets and slower southern/eastern adopters.

## III. Autonomous Driving & Mobility

### 7. Waymo’s ~4,000-vehicle software recall resurfaces: construction-zone gaps, fleet kept rolling (AV / US)
**Summary:** A Yahoo Autos Aug 9 recap notes Waymo filed an NHTSA software recall (26E035) covering nearly 4,000 robotaxis capable of driverless freeway operation after vehicles failed to recognize freeway construction zones and ramp closures in Phoenix and the Bay Area. The fix was delivered via over-the-air software; vehicles were not pulled from service. Business Insider the same week argued robotaxi firms are expanding faster than they can close reliability gaps, citing Waymo’s Dallas public opening, Zoox’s paid Las Vegas launch, and Tesla’s growing Robotaxi footprint.

### 8. Zoox paid robotaxi countdown: fares begin Aug 10 in Las Vegas (Mobility / US)
**Summary:** Amazon-owned Zoox will begin charging for rides in its steering-wheel-free, purpose-built robotaxis in Las Vegas on Aug 10, ending nearly a year of free public rides. The commercial step rests on an NHTSA temporary exemption (through July 2028, up to about 2,500 vehicles per year). Fares combine base, distance, and time, with destination fees possible for hubs such as the airport; quotes are shown before booking, and riders are not charged extra if the vehicle takes a longer route. Uber-app booking is planned later.

**Links:**

- [Axios — Amazon's Zoox's big bet on robotaxis starts in Las Vegas](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/05/zooxs-amazon-robotaxis-vegas)

**Commentary:** The first U.S. paid rides in a purpose-built, no-steering-wheel robotaxi mark the shift from demo economics to unit-economics scrutiny.

### 9. BYD and Sinopec convert a Shanghai petrol station into a Flash Charging flagship (Charging / China)
**Summary:** CarNewsChina and TechNode report that BYD and Sinopec remade a Shanghai petrol station into a Flash Charging hub: fuel operations ended, the Easy Joy convenience store remains, and about 12 charging stalls plus a rest area were added. BYD’s flash chargers use blade-battery buffers to ease grid draw and claim roughly five minutes from 10% to 70% charge.

### 10. Pennsylvania advances community-focused NEVI charger funding (Charging / US)
**Summary:** The Philadelphia Inquirer reported on Aug 9 that Pennsylvania is moving the latest wave of a roughly $5 billion federal EV-charger program into community sites. The state is allocated about $171 million overall; roughly $60 million is spent or planned, and about $100 million is obligated to community-focused grants. Unlike earlier interstate corridor builds, this round targets small towns hoping chargers also draw visitors; PennDOT expects municipal awards to begin this fall, with siting work continuing into next summer.
