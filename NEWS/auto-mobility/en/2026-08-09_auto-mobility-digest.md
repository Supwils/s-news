### 1. Hana Securities cites SNE: BYD’s H1 Europe sales ~185,000 units, ahead of Tesla (Market / Europe)
**Summary:** Asia Business Daily reported on Aug 9 that Hana Securities, citing SNE Research, said BYD sold about 39,000 vehicles in Europe in June (up ~60% year over year) and about 185,000 in the first half, surpassing Tesla’s roughly 172,000 over the same period. BYD’s overseas sales mix expanded from about 22.5% of total sales in Q2 last year to about 42.5% in Q2 this year; EU tariffs on Chinese PHEVs remain a near-term profitability risk.
