## I. Space & Deep Space

### 1. Nature: Jupiter-mass “exosatellite” around brown dwarf CD-35 2722 challenges what counts as a moon
**Summary:** On Jul 22, 2026, Kevin Hoy (ESO / Universidad Diego Portales and collaborators) reported in *Nature* radial-velocity evidence from ESO’s VLT/CRIRES+ for a companion to the directly imaged brown dwarf CD-35 2722 B. The best-fitting model points to a satellite with a minimum mass of about 0.9 Jupiter masses and an orbital period of roughly 170 days. The host star has about half the Sun’s mass and lies about 73 light-years away; the brown dwarf itself exceeds roughly 30 Jupiter masses. Because the object orbits a brown dwarf rather than a planet, the team calls it an exosatellite, and whether it qualifies as the first confirmed exomoon remains contested.

**Links:**

- [Nature — Planetary-mass exosatellite around CD-35 2722 B](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10751-w)
- [ESO / phys.org — Jupiter-mass ‘exomoon’ challenges cosmic labels](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-jupiter-mass-exomoon-orbiting-brown.html)

**Commentary:** The real leap is applying the first-exoplanet radial-velocity method to a brown-dwarf companion—taxonomy fights will linger, but the detection path is open.

### 3. BridgeBio: FDA accepts NDA for encaleret in ADH1; PDUFA date set for May 8, 2027
**Summary:** On Jul 22, 2026, BridgeBio Pharma said the U.S. FDA has accepted for filing its New Drug Application for encaleret, an oral negative modulator of the calcium-sensing receptor, for autosomal dominant hypocalcemia type 1 (ADH1). The Prescription Drug User Fee Act target action date is May 8, 2027, and the agency is not currently planning an advisory committee meeting. The filing rests on Phase 3 CALIBRATE, which met all pre-specified primary and key secondary endpoints, with data showing simultaneous restoration of blood and urine calcium and physiologic parathyroid hormone production. If approved, encaleret could become the first therapy specifically indicated for ADH1; pediatric CALIBRATE-PEDS and chronic hypoparathyroidism studies are advancing.

### 5. Nature Nanotechnology: Computationally guided protein–gold nanostructure clears amyloid-β aggregates
**Summary:** Qi et al., published online Jul 21, 2026 in *Nature Nanotechnology*, describe Aβ3FTnAu, a protein–gold hybrid nanostructure that embeds Aβ-recognition motifs in self-assembled human ferritin nanocages containing 12-atom gold clusters (Au–Au distances about 2.4–4.5 Å) to selectively recognize and disassemble human Aβ aggregates. Structural, mutagenesis, and simulation work implicate gold-coordinating residues H118, T122, and C130 interacting with Aβ Met35. In 5×FAD mice, systemic dosing reduced cerebral amyloid burden, preserved synapses, and improved cognition. The authors present it as a rationally designed bioactive nanomaterial for remodeling pathological protein aggregates.

**Links:**

- [Nature Nanotechnology — Aβ3FTnAu for amyloid-β clearance](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41565-026-02225-x)

**Commentary:** Computation plus metal-cluster chemistry sketches a clearance path beyond monoclonal antibodies—delivery, immunogenicity, and off-target toxicity still gate human translation.

## III. Climate, Energy & Environment

### 7. End-Triassic fern savannahs fueled continental-scale wildfire feedbacks
**Summary:** Led by Utrecht University geologists, a *Nature Geoscience* paper dated Jul 21, 2026—widely covered on Jul 22—links end-Triassic mass extinction wildfires to fern savannahs across Northwest Europe. About 201 million years ago, CAMP volcanism released vast CO₂ and warmed the planet by an estimated 5–10°C; tree forests collapsed and ferns spread. Combining microcharcoal, pyrolytic PAHs, and about 15,000 Palynomorph Darkness Index measurements from four drill cores, the team identifies an anomalous “Dark Zone” synchronized with the fern spike, pointing to frequent surface fires. Ferns’ ability to resprout after burning may have sustained a fire–fuel feedback for roughly 40,000 to 300,000 years.

**Links:**

- [Nature Geoscience — Fern savannah wildfires at end-Triassic](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-026-02048-4)
- [ScienceDaily — Ancient ferns turned Triassic Europe into wildfire inferno](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/07/260722032105.htm)

**Commentary:** Deep time offers a blunt analogy: warming plus forest collapse plus opportunistic species can manufacture a near-permanent fire season.

### 8. CAS Institute of Atmospheric Physics: Atmospheric transport strongly reshapes Northern Hemisphere CO₂ seasonal amplitude
**Summary:** On Jul 22, 2026, Jiawen Zhu’s team at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, reported a multi-observation and GEOS-Chem analysis quantifying how atmospheric transport redistributes CO₂ seasonality. In northern mid-latitudes (about 20°N–50°N), transport can explain more than 50% of seasonal CO₂ variation—exceeding terrestrial ecosystems—with mid-latitudes as the strongest seasonal sources and the tropics as sinks. Across 68 stations and 27 model sets, high-latitude seasonal CO₂ amplitude has strengthened since the 1960s and recently accelerated by about 74.24%, with terrestrial fluxes and transport contributing about 45.60% and 40.91%; tropical and Southern Hemisphere trends hinge more on transport-driven decadal turns.

**Links:**

**Commentary:** Separating “plant breathing” from “circulation shipping” keeps carbon-cycle diagnostics from mistaking remote transport for local source–sink shifts.

### 9. National Central University: Circumglobal wave pattern prolongs Northwestern Pacific marine heatwaves
**Summary:** Taiwanese coverage on Jul 22, 2026 highlighted work by Jin-Yi Yu (National Central University) and Yu Zhao (UC Irvine) arguing that multi-year Northwestern Pacific marine heatwaves are not merely local high-pressure/low-cloud events. Integrating more than 150 years of observations and dozens of climate models, they link prolonged events to a circumglobal wave (CGW) that relays energy across Europe, Asia, the North Pacific, and the North Atlantic, plus a self-reinforcing feedback as summer CGW activity strengthened since the 1970s. Published in *Science Advances*, the authors say embedding cross-basin coupling could improve seasonal forecasts of heatwave duration.

### 10. Nature Communications: Open-loop flow battery stores energy while making chemicals; modeled LCOE can be negative
**Summary:** Miao et al., published Jul 22, 2026 in *Nature Communications*, report an open-loop redox flow battery that couples VO₂⁺ reduction to organic oxidation during discharge and VO₂⁺ oxidation to hydrogen evolution during charge, working across 13 organic substrates. For ethylene glycol-to-glycolic acid with H₂ co-production, voltage and energy efficiencies can exceed 100% over more than 200 cycles; a PV-coupled day-charge/night-discharge demo made about 532.2 g of glycolic acid and 272.9 L of H₂ in 10 days. Techno-economic analysis suggests a profitable case with a levelized cost of electricity near −0.26 $/kWh, diversifying flow-battery revenue beyond peak–off-peak spreads.

**Links:**

- [Nature Communications — Redox flow battery coupling storage and chemicals](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75866-0)

**Commentary:** Storage hardware is moonlighting as a chemical plant—negative LCOE is a paper scenario, but dual “power + product” cash flows deserve serious grid modeling.

## IV. Fundamental & Cross-Disciplinary Research

### 11. AI-assisted counterexample shakes the Jacobian conjecture: non-invertible 3D map with constant Jacobian determinant
**Summary:** Harvard mathematician Levent Alpöge, affiliated with Anthropic, used Claude Fable 5 to find a counterexample to the 1939 Jacobian conjecture, drawing sustained science-media coverage on Jul 21–22, 2026. The short polynomial map in three complex dimensions has constant Jacobian determinant −2 yet sends multiple inputs to one output, so it is not invertible; reports put the example at about 216 characters, easing independent symbolic checks. The result falsifies the conjecture for all dimensions n ≥ 3 while leaving n = 2 open. Several mathematicians say the arithmetic has been independently verified, but a full write-up, prompting details, and journal peer review are still pending.

**Links:**

### 12. Carbon nanodots achieve minute-scale red phosphorescence with a 353-second lifetime
**Summary:** Ding et al., published Jul 22, 2026 in *Nature Communications*, report red phosphorescent carbon nanodots engineered via excited-state design and machine-learning optimization. The materials reach a phosphorescence lifetime of 353 seconds, with emission persistence beyond 4,000 seconds. Spectroscopy indicates a fast ³(n, π*)→¹n² channel coexisting with a slow ³(π, π*)→¹(π, π*) channel that dominates the ultralong glow. As a proof of concept, the team demonstrated a wearable passive LED indicator that remains visible for a limited time after power-off, targeting optoelectronic and bio-related uses.

**Links:**

- [Nature Communications — Minute-scale red phosphorescence in carbon nanodots](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76003-7)

**Commentary:** Pushing afterglow from seconds to minutes opens low-power signaling options—stability and manufacturing consistency remain the engineering gate.
