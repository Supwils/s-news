## I. Fundamental & Particle Physics

### 1. BESIII confirms X(2370) as glueball-dominated: a landmark test of a 50-year QCD prediction (Particle Physics)
**Summary:** At a special plenary of ICHEP 2026 in Natal, Brazil, on August 5, the BESIII Collaboration at the Beijing Electron Positron Collider (~700 scientists from 15 countries) reported that after about 15 years of work the dominant constituent of X(2370) is a pseudoscalar glueball with spin-parity 0⁻⁺. Discovered in J/ψ decays in 2011, the state had its quantum numbers fixed in 2024 with a sample of ~10 billion J/ψ events, matching lattice QCD; new decay modes further established its flavor-singlet nature, completing a mass–quantum-number–flavor-singlet evidence chain. A preprint is available as arXiv:2607.20366; international coverage intensified on August 8–9.

**Links:**

- [IHEP CAS — BESIII Identifies X(2370) as Glueball Dominated](https://english.ihep.cas.cn/nw/han/y26/202608/t20260804_1186878.html)

**Commentary:** Glueballs are matter made only of force carriers—this is the cleanest low-energy check yet of QCD’s non-Abelian structure.

### 2. Sunlight-pumped SPDC generates polarization-entangled photons at ~94% fidelity (Quantum Optics)
**Summary:** Teams led by Robert Boyd at the University of Ottawa and Hanieh Fattahi at the Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light report in *Optica* that natural sunlight, focused by an all-glass solar concentrator into a nonlinear crystal, can drive spontaneous parametric down-conversion and produce polarization-entangled photon pairs. Outdoor quantum-state tomography found ~94% fidelity to an ideal entangled state and a Bell-inequality violation; generation rates, when normalized, are comparable to laser-pumped setups. The result challenges the long-held assumption that entanglement sources require highly coherent lasers and points toward lower-power applications such as spaceborne quantum key distribution.

**Links:**

- [Optica — Researchers generate quantum entanglement using sunlight](https://www.optica.org/about/newsroom/news_releases/2026/researchers_generate_quantum_entanglement_using_sunlight/)
- [Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light — Quantum entanglement generated by sunlight](https://mpl.mpg.de/news/article/quantum-entanglement-generated-by-sunlight-for-the-first-time)

**Commentary:** Skipping electrical-to-optical conversion reframes both the energy budget and deployability of photonic quantum sources.

### 3. Correlated electrons push HHG into the water window at 280 eV, beyond the single-electron limit (Ultrafast Physics)
**Summary:** A study published in *Nature Photonics* on August 7 experimentally shows that strongly correlated electron pairs in helium can drive high-harmonic generation beyond the conventional single-active-electron cutoff. A weak secondary plateau extends emission from roughly 120 eV up to the water window near 280 eV, with a new cutoff scaling of up to ~5.5 times the ponderomotive energy versus the classic factor of 3.2. The findings recast HHG as a photonic probe of attosecond-to-femtosecond electron correlations and open paths to harder table-top X-ray sources and spectroscopy of correlated materials.

**Links:**

- [Nature Photonics — Correlated electrons extend X-ray HHG beyond the single-electron limit](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41566-026-01976-2)

**Commentary:** A higher cutoff is not only “harder X-rays”—it makes electron correlation itself a measurable ultrafast observable.

## III. Biomedicine

### 5. FDA approves Orzeyful (oveporexton): first oral OX2R agonist targeting NT1’s orexin deficiency (Medicine)
**Summary:** On August 5, Takeda announced U.S. FDA approval of ORZEYFUL (oveporexton, formerly TAK-861) for adults with narcolepsy type 1 (narcolepsy with cataplexy). The oral orexin receptor 2 (OX2R) agonist is positioned as the first U.S.-indicated medicine designed to treat NT1’s underlying orexin deficiency holistically rather than isolated symptoms. Commercial availability awaits DEA controlled-substance scheduling (expected within about 90 days); Phase 3 programs including FirstLight and RadiantLight support the indication for an estimated ~120,000 U.S. adults with NT1.

## IV. Climate & Environment

### 8. *Science*: Siberian methane nearly doubled in a decade; high-emissions path could offset ~20% of anthropogenic cuts (Climate)
**Summary:** An international team led by Liu Yi at the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, published in *Science* on August 6 that Siberian CH₄ emissions rose by 12.0±1.9 Tg from 2010–2023 (~5%/yr, 1.1±0.1 Tg/yr)—about 92% of the concurrent rise in global wetland methane—after GOSAT and tall-tower assimilation cut uncertainty to ~10%. Western Siberia’s wetter pathway favors methanogenesis; eastern Siberia’s warm-dry regime plus wildfires and abrupt permafrost thaw dominate growth. Under SSP5-8.5, the 2050 increase versus the 2010–2023 mean could offset roughly one-fifth of anthropogenic methane reductions needed for climate targets.

### 9. Global butterfly range shifts: 80% expand; 79% of shifts tied to climate and extremes (Ecology)
**Summary:** Researchers from Monash, iDiv, the University of Jena, and UFZ published on August 8 in *Nature Ecology & Evolution* the largest synthesis of butterfly range shifts: 6,182 records for 1,758 species (~10% of known diversity) across 105 countries. About 80% of species expanded ranges, 27% contracted, and 22% shifted elevationally; 79% of recorded shifts were linked to climate change and extreme weather. Horizontal expansions appear on every continent and are especially numerous in the tropics; contractions concentrate in temperate Europe and North America; elevational moves are almost exclusive to the Northern Hemisphere. Major gaps remain in Central Africa, Southeast Asia, New Guinea, and Amazonia.

**Links:**

- [Nature Ecology & Evolution — Extensive climate-induced range shifts in butterflies](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41559-026-03117-y)
- [phys.org — Butterfly ranges shift worldwide amid climate change](https://phys.org/news/2026-08-butterfly-ranges-shift-worldwide-climate.html)

**Commentary:** Butterflies are early-warning biota—expansion is not safety, and under-monitored tropics may hide the deepest losses.

### 10. Arctic Ocean acidification persists under negative emissions: ~120 ppm extra CO₂ drop needed to exit aragonite corrosivity (Ocean)
**Summary:** A *Nature Climate Change* paper published August 6 analyzes acidity ([H⁺]) and aragonite saturation (ΩArag) in eight Earth system models under rising-then-falling CO₂ and identifies the Arctic as a hotspot of delayed reversibility. Under falling CO₂, Arctic surface waters remain comparatively more acidic, and aragonite-corrosive conditions (ΩArag<1) persist until atmospheric CO₂ falls ~120 ppm below the threshold at which they first appeared on the way up. The hysteresis stems from erosion of a sea-ice–maintained dissolved inorganic carbon deficit that is not fully restored as ice recovers during CO₂ decline.

**Links:**

- [Nature Climate Change — Persistence of Arctic Ocean acidification under negative emissions](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41558-026-02715-9)

**Commentary:** Negative emissions are not a rewind button—Arctic chemistry remembers longer than the atmospheric CO₂ curve.

## V. Quantum Materials & Devices

### 11. Encapsulation epitaxy yields inch-scale air-stable monolayer NbSe₂ for superconducting circuits (Materials)
**Summary:** A *Nature* paper dated August 5 reports “encapsulation epitaxy”: graphene or hexagonal boron nitride pre-deposited on SiO₂/Si₃N₄ templates both the growth of monolayer NbSe₂ at the encapsulation–substrate interface and protects it from ambient oxidation, enabling large-area (>1 inch), air-stable films. The graphene/NbSe₂ heterostructures show superconductivity with Tc≈1 K, enhanced charge-density-wave transition near 177 K, and kinetic inductance around 1.7 nH□⁻¹—attractive for compact high-inductance superconducting circuit elements. The route points toward wafer-scale, monolithic van der Waals superconducting devices.

**Links:**

- [Nature — Encapsulation epitaxy of air-stable 2D superconductors for quantum circuits](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10865-1)

**Commentary:** Air stability has been the manufacturing choke point for 2D superconductors—encapsulation turns protection into the growth condition itself.
