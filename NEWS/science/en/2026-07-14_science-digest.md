### 3. Astronomers Detect First Sugar Molecule—Erythrulose—in the Interstellar Medium
**Summary:** phys.org reported on July 13, 2026, on a *Nature Astronomy* paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41550-026-02905-7) from Izaskun Jiménez-Serra's team at Spain's Center for Astrobiology. Using ultrabroadband surveys with the Yebes 40 m and IRAM 30 m radio telescopes toward the Galactic Centre cloud G+0.693−0.027, the group matched 12 spectral lines to laboratory erythrulose—the only possible four-carbon ketose—marking the first true sugar detected in interstellar space. Erythrulose is at least eight times more abundant than analogous three-carbon sugars in the same region. Quantum-chemical and astrochemical models suggest formation on interstellar ice grains from two-carbon precursors such as glycolaldehyde and ethylene glycol rather than traditional one-carbon-at-a-time growth. The team estimates 0.5–50 million metric tons could have reached Earth's surface during the Late Heavy Bombardment, supporting an exogenous sugar supply for origin-of-life studies.

**Links:**

- [phys.org — Study reports the first detection of a sugar in interstellar space](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-sugar-interstellar-space.html)
- [Nature Astronomy — Detection of a four-carbon sugar in interstellar space](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-026-02905-7)

**Commentary:** The prebiotic inventory now includes sugars—if more complex sugars follow, the panspermia narrative may extend from amino acids and bases to carbohydrates that could feed early metabolism directly.

### 4. Hubble and Webb Find Omega Centauri's First Stellar-Mass Black Hole with Record 94-Year Orbit
**Summary:** NASA Science announced on July 13, 2026, alongside an *Astrophysical Journal Letters* paper (DOI: 10.3847/2041-8213/ae7a5c) that Matthew Whitaker and colleagues used more than 20 years of Hubble archival data from the oMEGACat project plus new Webb observations to astrometrically detect a visible main-sequence star orbiting an invisible compact companion—stellar-mass black hole oMEGACat BH-2, with mass ~4.46 solar masses, period ~94 years, and semimajor axis ~31 AU. This is the longest-period black-hole binary known and the first astrometrically confirmed stellar-mass black hole in a globular cluster. Simulations favor dynamic formation in the crowded cluster, with an expected disruption timescale of ~800 Myr.

**Links:**

- [NASA Science — NASA's Hubble Discovers First of Star Cluster's Missing Black Holes](https://science.nasa.gov/missions/hubble/nasas-hubble-discovers-first-of-star-clusters-missing-black-holes/)
- [The Astrophysical Journal Letters — A Long Period Stellar-mass Black Hole Binary in ω Centauri](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2041-8213/ae7a5c)

**Commentary:** The Omega Centauri "missing black hole" census has only turned its first page—a 94-year orbit demands generational follow-up, but it anchors theory on black-hole formation in low-metallicity environments.

**Summary:** Per AstraZeneca's July 14, 2026 RNS filing and a Dizal press release the same day, AstraZeneca signed an exclusive global license for Zegfrovy (sunvozertinib), an oral irreversible EGFR inhibitor. ### 6. Cambridge AI-Designed Pan-Sarbecovirus DNA Vaccine pEVAC-PS Completes Phase I with Needle-Free Delivery
**Summary:** Per EurekAlert! and a *Journal of Infection* paper (DOI: 10.1016/j.jinf.2026.106759), the University of Cambridge and spin-out DIOSynVax completed the first human trial of a vaccine whose antigen was fully computer-designed: pEVAC-PS Phase I dose escalation (ISRCTN87813400) enrolled 39 healthy adults aged 18–50 at NIHR sites in Southampton and Cambridge for four dose levels (0.2–1.2 mg) on days 0 and 28 via needle-free microfluidic intradermal DNA delivery. A larger Phase II trial is planned to test broad protective efficacy.

## III. Climate & Environment

### 8. Glycerol-Fed Bacteria Fix 95% of Dissolved Uranium in Mine Water over 130 Days as Stable FeU(V)O₄
**Summary:** ScienceAlert reported on July 14, 2026, on a *Nature Communications* paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-72560-z) from Germany's HZDR, Wismut GmbH, and the University of Granada showing natural mine-water microbes, with glycerol as a carbon source, simultaneously reduce dissolved hexavalent uranium to tetravalent biogenic uraninite nanoparticles and rare pentavalent forms that combine with iron and oxygen as FeU(V)O₄ nanoparticles and U(V)-carbonate complexes. After 130 days under anoxic conditions, only ~5% of dissolved uranium remained; pentavalent uranium persisted through four weeks of oxygen exposure, challenging the conventional U(VI)→U(IV) direct-precipitation picture and offering new bioremediation mechanisms.

### 9. Northeast Pacific Iceberg Meltwater Can Remotely Weaken the Atlantic Overturning Circulation
**Summary:** phys.org reported on July 13, 2026, on a *Nature Communications* paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-75199-y) from UC Davis's Chijun Sun team combining paleoclimate data and freshwater-hosing simulations. Northeast Pacific "Siku" iceberg-discharge events at Heinrich stadial onsets can transport freshwater to North Atlantic deep-water formation regions, diluting surface waters, triggering subsurface warming, and weakening the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC), then amplifying melt from the British and Laurentide ice sheets. The mechanism offers a Pacific-driven trigger independent of North Atlantic icebergs; Sun's prior work warns 21st-century AMOC weakening could nearly halve annual precipitation in Central America, the Amazon, and West Africa.

### 10. Chinese Perovskite–Organic Tandem Cell Hits Certified 28.04% Efficiency, Retains 90% after 625 Hours
**Summary:** Xinhua reported on July 14, 2026, on a *Nature* paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10869-x, online July 13) from Li Yongfang and Meng Lei at the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Chemistry. A photo-transformable additive TDB enables two-stage control of wide-bandgap mixed-halide perovskites—improving halide homogeneity during crystallization and forming stronger grain-boundary adsorbates under operation to suppress photo-induced halide segregation. A single-junction wide-bandgap cell reached 20.01% efficiency with 1.42 V open-circuit voltage; a monolithic perovskite–organic tandem achieved 28.80% with a certified steady-state efficiency of 28.04%, retaining 90% of initial performance after 625 hours under the ISOS-L-1 protocol—a record for this device class.

### 11. KAIST and MIT Demo Electrically Reconfigurable Mid-Infrared Space-Light Modulator Toward Software-Defined Space Sensors
**Summary:** EurekAlert! announced on July 14, 2026, on a *Nature Communications* paper published online July 7 that KAIST aerospace engineering professor Hyun Jung Kim and MIT professor Juejun Hu built the first transmissive mid-infrared amplitude-only spatial light modulator on a scalable two-dimensional electrically addressable metasurface. The paper, "Two-Dimensional Pixel-Level Addressable Mid-Infrared Metasurface Spatial Light Modulator" (DOI: 10.1038/s41467-026-75346-5), lays groundwork for programmable space optical payloads.

### 12. Regensburg Team Tracks Tunneling Electrons at the Attosecond–Angstrom Limit
**Summary:** phys.org reported in July 2026 on a *Nature Photonics* paper (DOI: 10.1038/s41566-026-01932-0) from Jascha Repp, Rupert Huber, and colleagues at Germany's Regensburg Center for Ultrafast Nanoscopy (RUN) with Angel Rubio's group at the Max Planck Institute in Hamburg. Lightwave-driven scanning tunneling microscopy now resolves isolated electron tunneling transients shorter than 1 femtosecond and localizes tunneling wave packets at angstrom scale on copper adatoms; full quantum simulations match experiments and show ~500-attosecond delay between the electron and the light field. Authors say the platform can probe wavefunction dynamics inside atoms, molecules, and solids and may trigger and observe chemical reactions at relevant length and time scales.

**Links:**

**Commentary:** From "seeing electrons" to "seeing when and where they tunnel"—real-time movies of bond making and breaking may move from conceptual demos to repeatable experiments.
