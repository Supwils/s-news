### 4. Treasury/IRS expand the 45Q safe harbor (Notice 2026-50) for CCS and enhanced oil and gas compliance
**Summary:** The US Treasury Department and the IRS issued Notice 2026-50, expanding and extending the safe harbor for the federal Section 45Q carbon capture tax credit. The notice provides an alternative compliance pathway: if the EPA electronic greenhouse-gas reporting system is unavailable by the applicable deadline after the reporting year, projects may complete reporting using certification via independent engineers or geologists. It also expands the scope to qualified carbon dioxide used in enhanced oil and natural gas recovery projects, reducing uncertainty from reporting-system availability issues.

**Links:**

- [Engineering News-Record (ENR) — Treasury, IRS Extend 45Q Safe Harbor as EPA Weighs Reporting Rollback](https://www.enr.com/articles/63503-treasury-irs-extend-45q-safe-harbor-as-epa-weighs-reporting-rollback)

**Commentary:** When data-reporting systems may fail, safe-harbor pathways help convert credit eligibility from interpretation risk into more predictable deliverability.

### 6. US 24 GW storage ambition meets “FEOC” supply-chain gate: 55% non-prohibited foreign-cost share for 2026 starters
**Summary:** Reporting indicates the US expects about 24 GW of utility-scale battery storage additions in 2026, but new federal rules make financing and supply-chain matching a key variable. Under the “One Big Beautiful Bill Act,” foreign-entity-of-concern (FEOC) restrictions require that for projects beginning construction in 2026, at least 55% of costs must originate from non-prohibited foreign entities to qualify for the investment tax credit (ITC). The threshold rises to 75% for projects beginning construction in 2030 and after; compliant projects keep access to the credit for a longer runway, but the central challenge remains sourcing batteries and critical minerals from eligible suppliers.

**Links:**

- [gcn.com — U.S. battery storage developers plan a record 24 gigawatts in 2026 as new supply-chain rules bite](https://gcn.com/battery-storage-developers-plan-record-gigawatts/20256/)

**Commentary:** When tax credits are tied to verifiable supply chains, build curves start asking “what can be sourced,” not just “what can be sold.”

### 12. Deadly floods in India’s Assam: over 100 deaths; experts call for better warnings and long-term planning
