**Summary:** People's Daily reported on **July 11, 2026** that the National Energy Administration's *Energy Sector Energy-Saving and Decarbonization Action Plan (2026–2028)* targets an average annual increase of about **1 percentage point** in the non-fossil energy consumption share by **2028**, a **15-percentage-point** rise in coal-power capacity meeting current efficiency benchmarks, and batches of zero-carbon coal/oil zones and parks. ### 3. North Macedonia's Standalone Renewables Law Takes Effect **July 10**, Aligning with EU RED III and Market Coupling (Policy · Southeast Europe)
**Summary:** Karanovic & Partners and Balkan Green Energy News reported **July 10–11, 2026** that North Macedonia's Law on the Use of Energy from Renewable Sources entered into force on **July 10**, creating a standalone (Lex specialis) framework aligned with EU Directive **2018/2001** and RED III. The law introduces competitive support auctions, market premiums, renewable PPAs, net metering/billing, renewable energy communities, and cross-border guarantees-of-origin trading. The government notified the Energy Community Secretariat that legal alignment for EU electricity market coupling is complete, entering joint verification with the European Commission. Officials say the reform reduces import dependence, improves investment predictability, and enables citizen and corporate distributed generation.

### 4. Spain Advances Draft Royal Decree on Wind Repowering and Industrial Self-Consumption, Releasing Grid Node Capacity (Policy · Spain)
**Summary:** Osborne Clarke reported on **July 10, 2026** that Spain's Ministry for Ecological Transition (MITECO) opened public consultation on a draft Royal Decree through **July 13**, building on crisis-response decrees **7/2026** and **18/2026**. Measures aim to release grid-node capacity, simplify permitting, and increase renewables' contribution to system services. A core mechanism releases capacity reserved for tenders to renewable self-consumption projects tied to industrial loads, with a **two-year** application window after entry into force. The draft also updates **2013** voltage-control rules requiring renewables to actively support grid voltage, reducing fossil-plant reliance, and regulates solar-thermal expansion and ramp-limit monitoring. Industry is watching whether this can accelerate aging wind-farm repowering and ease industrial power costs.

### 6. Bipartisan Washington Push for "American Energy Acceleration Act" Would Compress NEPA Reviews to Two Years (Policy · U.S.)
