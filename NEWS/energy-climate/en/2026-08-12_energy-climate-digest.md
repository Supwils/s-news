### 1. IEA August OMR: 2026 global oil demand now seen down 1.6 mb/d as Hormuz closure persists (Policy · Global)
**Summary:** The International Energy Agency's August 2026 Oil Market Report, released Aug 12, cuts the 2026 global oil demand outlook to a 1.6 million barrel-per-day (mb/d) decline — 510,000 b/d deeper than July — citing the ongoing Strait of Hormuz closure and elevated fuel prices, while noting demand may return to growth in Q4. Global supply is now forecast to fall by an average 4.3 mb/d in 2026, 600,000 b/d worse than last month's estimate. Observed inventories dropped below 7.9 billion barrels in July for the first time since April 2025; the IEA warns buffer stocks are depleting rapidly and reopening the strait is increasingly urgent.

### 2. EU Battery Regulation fully applies Aug 12: industrial and storage batteries require carbon data and digital passport (Policy · EU)
**Summary:** Under the confirmed timeline of EU Regulation 2023/1542, industrial and energy storage batteries placed on the European market — including export storage products — must from Aug 12, 2026 carry certified carbon footprint declarations, lifecycle recycling-rate data, and machine-readable Battery Digital Passports (BDP). Exporters typically need EU authorized representatives, third-party LCA verification, and BDP platform connectivity; non-compliant shipments risk customs rejection. The rule turns carbon disclosure and supply-chain traceability into immediate market-access requirements.

### 3. India strengthens storage framework: INR 5,400 crore VGF for 30 GWh BESS plus full ISTS charge waiver (Policy · India)
**Summary:** In a written reply to India's Rajya Sabha reported around Aug 12, the Ministry of Power outlined roughly INR 5,400 crore in Viability Gap Funding through the Power System Development Fund to develop 30 GWh of battery energy storage, plus a 100% waiver of inter-state transmission system (ISTS) charges for eligible storage — full waiver for co-located BESS commissioned by June 2028 and pumped-hydro projects awarded by that date, tapering 25% per year thereafter. ### 7. Asia's first flexible-DC offshore wind project surpasses 10 TWh cumulative generation (Clean Power · China)
**Summary:** CCTV and China Economic Net reported Aug 12 that Three Gorges New Energy's 800 MW Jiangsu Rudong offshore wind project surpassed 10 billion kWh cumulative generation on Aug 11 — Asia's first offshore wind farm delivered via flexible DC transmission. Grid-connected in 2021 with Asia's first offshore VSC converter station, it has run over 1,600 days at 99.5% availability; line losses are about 1.2 percentage points lower than AC alternatives, saving over CNY 735 million in transmission costs. The clean output could supply roughly 4 million households for a year, equivalent to about 7.4 million tonnes of CO₂ avoided.
