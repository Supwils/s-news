## I. Policy & Carbon Markets

### 1. South Korea Caps Renewable Setbacks: 200m for Solar, 1,500m for Wind (Policy · Korea)
**Summary:** South Korea’s Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment said on August 11 that the Cabinet approved an amendment to the enforcement decree of the renewable energy promotion law, setting national caps on local setback rules that had varied widely. Solar facilities may be required to sit no more than 200 meters from residential clusters of five or more households, and road setbacks for solar are barred; wind setbacks are capped at 1,500 meters from homes and 500 meters from roads, with a safety floor of twice turbine height. Of 228 local governments, 129 regulate setbacks by ordinance; the Korea Energy Economics Institute estimates such rules have cut potential solar siting area by about 16,622 square kilometers. Related legal changes are slated to take effect around September 18.

### 3. China Issues Coal Industry 15th Five-Year Plan: 75% Intelligent Mine Capacity by 2030 (Policy · China)
**Summary:** China’s NDRC and National Energy Administration released the Coal Industry Development Plan for the 15th Five-Year Period, publicly detailed on August 10–11.
