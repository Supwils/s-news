## I. Policy & Grid Emergency

### 1. U.S. DOE issues emergency dispatch order for Southwest Power Pool across 17 states (Policy · Grid)
**Summary:** On Jul 26, 2026, the U.S. Department of Energy issued an emergency order at the request of Southwest Power Pool (SPP), directing SPP to dispatch specified generating units and authorizing backup generation as a last resort before or during an Energy Emergency Alert Level 3 (EEA 3) to reduce blackout risk in extreme heat. The order covers 17 states, took effect the same day, and expires at 11:59 p.m. CDT on Aug 3, 2026. DOE said more than 35 GW of unused backup generation remains available nationwide and cited National Laboratory estimates that outages cost Americans about $44 billion a year.

### 2. U.S. heat waves and data-center load push multiple grids toward tipping points (Grid · Climate)
**Summary:** Bloomberg and others reported on Jul 26 that back-to-back U.S. heat waves, with temperatures topping about 100°F (38°C), are driving air-conditioning demand while around-the-clock AI data centers shrink reserves and stress aging transmission. ## II. Clean Power, Storage & Hydrogen

### 3. Grenergy closes €100m non-recourse financing for Spain stand-alone BESS (Storage · Europe)
**Summary:** Spanish clean-energy developer Grenergy said on Jul 22 it closed about €100 million in senior non-recourse project financing with Banco Santander and Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC) for the 618 MWh Oviedo stand-alone battery project—described as Spain’s first bank financing of its kind for independent storage. Construction has begun beside the La Estrecha substation in La Corredoria, with commercial operation targeted for the first half of 2027; a 10-year financial tolling agreement from 2028 was signed earlier. The asset anchors Grenergy’s European Greenbox platform, which the company says has nearly 30 GWh under development across six markets.

### 6. North Sea PosHYdon pilot produces first green hydrogen on an operating gas platform (Hydrogen · Europe)
**Summary:** H2 Tech and others reported on Jul 26 that the PosHYdon pilot produced green hydrogen on Eni Energy Netherlands’ operating Q13a-A platform—described as the world’s first project combining offshore wind, offshore hydrogen production and natural-gas operations on an active platform. About 13 km off Scheveningen, Q13a-A is the first fully electrified production platform in the Dutch North Sea; seawater is demineralized then electrolyzed using offshore wind power. Tests will first stabilize operations, then probe electrolyzer response to wind variability, with key findings compiled in August–September and shared with industry in autumn.

**Links:**

- [H2 Tech — First production of green hydrogen on operational North Sea platform](https://h2-tech.com/news/2026/07-26/first-production-of-green-hydrogen-on-operational-north-sea-platform/)
