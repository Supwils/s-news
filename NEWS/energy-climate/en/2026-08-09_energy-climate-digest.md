## I. Climate Extremes & Power Stress

### 1. Record-low Rhine and Danube levels force European nuclear and hydro cuts (Disaster · Europe)
**Summary:** Europe’s drought has pushed the Rhine at Kaub to about 24 centimeters—its lowest since records began in 1880. Along the Danube, Romania has shut its sole Danube-cooled reactor and used naval underwater blasts to improve cooling flow; Hungary’s Paks plant (about 40% of national power) faces closure risk, and Serbia has cut hydropower. Kiel Institute economists estimate Rhine low water could shave German Q3 GDP by up to 0.2 percentage points and cost roughly €1–2 billion in value added.

### 2. Half of England and all of Wales enter official drought; over 10 million face water limits (Disaster · UK)
**Summary:** The UK government has declared drought across half of England and all of Wales. More than 10 million people face water restrictions and hosepipe bans. Officials link about 2,700 excess deaths to May–June heat; July was the driest on record in many areas, with parts of London seeing no rain. Farmers report potato yields may be halved and some dairy output down about 20%; forecasters say meaningful rain may not arrive until October.

### 4. Amazon confirms Texas GW Ranch off-grid gas plant, up to ~7.65 GW nameplate (Oil & Gas · AI power)
**Summary:** Cleanview reported Aug 7—and Amazon confirmed—that the company acquired the GW Ranch site in Pecos County, Texas, where Pacifico Energy’s on-site gas plant will power Amazon’s first major off-grid AI data center. ### 6. India Home Ministry bars new renewables within 1 km of LAC, LoC, and IB (Policy · India)
