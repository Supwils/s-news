## I. Policy & Regulation

### 1. EU finds Meta's Instagram/Facebook "addictive design" breaches the DSA, with fines up to 6% of global revenue (Regulation / Europe)
**Summary:** Per the European Commission and ABC News on July 10, 2026, the Commission issued preliminary findings that Meta's Instagram and Facebook violate the Digital Services Act (DSA) through addictive design — infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, and highly personalized recommendations — by failing to adequately assess risks to users' physical and mental health, especially minors and vulnerable adults, and by offering mitigation measures that do not effectively address those risks. The Commission is calling on Meta to disable autoplay and infinite scroll by default, implement effective screen-time breaks, and make recommender systems less engagement-driven; if the findings are confirmed, Meta could face fines of up to 6% of global annual turnover.
