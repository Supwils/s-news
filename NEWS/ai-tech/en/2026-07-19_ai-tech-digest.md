## I. Policy & Regulation

### 1. China’s AI companion rules bite: minors banned from virtual partners as emotional-dependency curbs take hold (Policy / China)
**Summary:** ABC News reported on Jul 19 that China’s newly effective rules ban tech firms from offering AI or virtual partners to minors, require platforms to curb excessive use, forbid chatbots from encouraging emotional reliance, and mandate crisis intervention when users show extreme distress. Earlier Caixin, CNA, and CGTN coverage confirmed the package—jointly issued by the Cyberspace Administration of China and four other agencies—as China’s first dedicated regime for anthropomorphic AI companion services. ByteDance’s Doubao, Alibaba’s Qwen, and Tencent’s Yuanbao have suspended or shut personalized companion/agent features. Social feeds filled with user “farewells,” underscoring how quickly consumer emotional-AI products hit compliance walls.

### 2. Trump administration reportedly speeds a FINRA-style watchdog for frontier-model safety reviews (Policy / US)
**Summary:** Multiple outlets citing Bloomberg say US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is advancing a plan for an independent AI oversight body modeled on FINRA and reporting to the SEC; White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles is reviewing it. The proposed process would screen for dangerous capabilities such as deception, bioweapons assistance, and malicious hacking, with frontier labs voluntarily submitting models roughly 30 days before public release. The push follows industry complaints about ad-hoc US interventions around Anthropic and OpenAI flagship releases. Forbes debated the idea on Jul 19. No legislation, funding, or timeline has been finalized.

**Links:**

- [Seoul Economic Daily — Trump speeds up AI self-regulation push](https://en.sedaily.com/international/2026/07/19/fearing-chinas-lead-trump-speeds-up-ai-self-regulation-push)
- [Forbes — Does the AI industry need its own FINRA?](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jamesbroughel/2026/07/19/does-the-ai-industry-need-its-own-finra/)

**Commentary:** Washington wants a self-regulatory shell to replace case-by-case crackdowns—the real test is whether voluntary pre-release review can bind actual frontier launches.
