## I. Policy & Regulation

### 1. Russia’s first AI law takes effect, with sovereign-model and labeling rules phased in (Policy)
**Summary:** TASS reported on Sep 1 that Russia’s first dedicated artificial-intelligence law entered into force, covering development, deployment and use of AI, defining core terms such as AI and large foundation models and their developers, and introducing general liability for sector violations. Some provisions—including certain presidential and federal executive powers, the split of “sovereign” versus “national” models by external-tech dependence and related developer duties, plus rules on labeling AI-generated content and training on intellectual-property works—only take effect on Mar 1, 2027. Support measures for developers of sovereign and national models apply from Sep 1, 2026.

**Links:**

- [TASS — Russia's first AI law comes into force](https://tass.com/society/2180163)

**Commentary:** Moscow is sequencing the statute—concepts and liability first, labeling and sovereignty tiers later—so industrial support and content/training compliance run on different clocks.

### 2. China’s micro-drama rules take effect, putting AI shorts into a three-tier licensing regime (China · Policy)
**Summary:** NRTA Order No. 16, the Measures for the Development and Administration of Micro-Dramas, took effect on Sep 1, 2026—China’s first dedicated departmental regulation for the format, spanning filing, licensing, platform duties, recommendation algorithms and AI use. Companion tiering standards apply the same day: sensitive themes are always Category I; for general themes, live-action projects at or above roughly RMB 3 million and AI micro-dramas at or above roughly RMB 800,000 also fall into Category I, requiring national filing plus provincial-or-higher review and an issuance license. Lower-budget titles rely on platform pre-broadcast review and program IDs. AI-generated micro-dramas must carry mandatory labels; titles that skip compliance cannot go live.

**Links:**

- [NRTA — Order No. 16: Measures for the Development and Administration of Micro-Dramas](https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2026/7/31/art_113_73785.html)
- [The Paper — Three-tier micro-drama oversight starts Sep 1](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33804282)

**Commentary:** Regulators turned theme sensitivity plus budget into enforceable gates—and priced AI shorts into the top tier with an explicit investment threshold.

### 5. Sony and Warner publishers sue Anthropic over alleged pirated lyrics used to train Claude (Litigation)
**Summary:** Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell (among others) filed suit on Aug 28 in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California against Anthropic, CEO Dario Amodei and co-founder Benjamin Mann; Fortune and peers amplified the case on Sep 1. The backdrop includes Anthropic’s roughly $1.5 billion authors settlement over pirated books and earlier music-industry cases against Claude.

## III. Models & Products

### 6. Runway unveils Solaris, an “Interface World Model” that renders interactive UIs frame by frame (Product)
**Summary:** AI video company Runway detailed Solaris around Sep 1 as the first system in a category it calls Interface World Models: instead of running a UI as code, it renders the interface live at roughly 720p and responds to clicks, drags or voice. Built on Gen-4.5, a language model decides how the interface changes while the world model draws each frame; Runway also frames it as a gym for agents that still fail when websites deviate from fixed layouts. The company says stable, readable text remains hard, long-session reliability is open, and assistive tech such as screen readers is unsupported—making Solaris a research and partner early-access effort for now.

**Links:**

- [THE DECODER — Runway's Solaris generates software interfaces in real time](https://the-decoder.com/runways-solaris-is-an-ai-system-that-generates-software-interfaces-in-real-time/)

**Commentary:** If interfaces can be generated instead of compiled, agents leave the DOM for a pixel world—until text is crisp, demos will outrun delivery.

## IV. Funding & Agent Security

### 9. AIR exits stealth with ~$50M led by Sequoia and Greenoaks for an inline agent firewall (Funding)
**Summary:** New York startup AIR on Sep 1 emerged from stealth with about $50 million led by Sequoia Capital and Greenoaks, with Swish Ventures and Netz Capital among other participants. AIR builds an inline firewall for AI agents that aims to block malicious instructions, untrusted data and compromised tools before they enter an agent’s context, plus discovery, governance and monitoring of runtime supply-chain pieces such as skills, plugins and MCPs. Founded in February 2026 by a security-heavy team, the company is betting that agents make software supply chains a first-order enterprise attack surface.
