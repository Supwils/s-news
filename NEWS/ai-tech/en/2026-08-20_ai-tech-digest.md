## I. Policy and Industry Deployment

### 1. China's Ministry of Transport briefs on “AI + Transportation”: 860 scenarios, first 41 build guides, and a “1+N+X” model stack (policy)

**Summary:** On August 20, China's Ministry of Transport held a press briefing on its “AI + Transportation” typical application-scenario innovation campaign. Science and Technology Department head Xu Wenqiang said the ministry is focusing on four directions—infrastructure, vehicles and equipment, transport services, and industry governance—and has mapped 860 priority breakthrough scenarios across rail, road, waterway, aviation, and postal services, while accelerating a “1+N+X” integrated transportation large-model system. An action plan issued in June defines three scenario types (promotion, demonstration, and hard-tech breakthroughs); the first batch of 41 key scenario build guides covers ten directions including intelligent driving, smart highways, smart railways, smart civil aviation, smart postal services, and intelligent multimodal logistics. The State Post Bureau said more than 16,000 unmanned delivery vehicles and over 400 drones are already in end-mile use; the ministry is also advancing a high-quality dataset program and has published application-security guidance.

### 2. Alibaba earnings: AI cloud & compute up 45%; AI-related products post a 12th straight quarter of triple-digit growth (earnings)

**Summary:** On August 20, Alibaba reported results for the quarter ended June 30, 2026: revenue of about RMB 268.953 billion, up 9% year over year. The newly highlighted “AI cloud and compute services” segment brought in about RMB 48.437 billion, up 45%; AI-related product revenue reached about RMB 12.376 billion, marking a 12th consecutive quarter of triple-digit growth, with adjusted EBITA of about RMB 5.628 billion, up 133%. Capex hit about RMB 67.678 billion, up 75%, largely for AI infrastructure.

## II. M&A, Funding, and Infrastructure

### 3. Stripe agrees to acquire AI model gateway OpenRouter: 400+ models; reports put the deal near $7.5B (M&A)

**Summary:** On August 19, Stripe announced an agreement to acquire OpenRouter, an AI model gateway and routing platform. Stripe’s newsroom post says OpenRouter helps businesses route tokens across 400+ models from more than 80 providers based on task complexity, price, speed, and reliability. OpenRouter’s blog says it processes 10+ trillion tokens per day for a community of over 10 million developers and companies, and that product, mission, and current commitments remain unchanged pending a close in the coming weeks. Financial terms were not disclosed; CNBC and TechCrunch, citing The New York Times, report a price near $7.5 billion, including about $1.5 billion for the founders, after a ~$1.3 billion valuation raise of $113 million less than three months earlier.

### 4. India's Razorpay launches Vulcan, a payments foundation model claiming higher success rates and stronger cross-border fraud detection (product)

**Summary:** Indian payments firm Razorpay announced Vulcan, a transformer-based foundation model built for payments with NVIDIA and AWS, aiming to unify routing, risk, and checkout personalization in one intelligence layer. ## III. Autonomous Driving and Robotaxi

### 5. The Verge: Austin Tesla Robotaxi rides monitored over two weeks were all unsupervised, ahead of Cybercab (product)

**Summary:** On August 20, The Verge reported that crowdsourced Robotaxi Tracker logged 170 Tesla Robotaxi rides in Austin over the past two weeks—all unsupervised—across 54 different cars, with unsupervised fleets also rising in Dallas and Houston. Tracker creator Ethan McKanna said Austin appears to have stopped supervised rides for a couple of weeks. The shift comes as Tesla prepares to introduce the wheel- and pedal-less Cybercab in Austin as soon as this month. Even so, Waymo still operates more than 300 driverless vehicles in Austin alone and about 4,000 across other cities, leaving Tesla far behind on scale. Tesla did not respond to requests for comment.

### 6. Uber launches its first European AV bookings in Zagreb via Pony.ai tech and Verne ops—with a safety operator onboard (product)

**Summary:** On August 19, Uber, Pony.ai, and Verne announced that Zagreb, Croatia, is the first European city where riders can book an autonomous vehicle through the Uber app. Uber’s release says Pony.ai supplies the AV stack, Verne owns and operates the fleet, and Uber integrates the rider experience; at launch a licensed operator monitors from behind the wheel as part of a phased path to fully driverless service. Coverage starts in key Zagreb areas including the city center and is set to expand; the partners say they plan additional European cities. Some secondary reports cite targets around 2,000 Pony.ai robotaxis across five European cities, but Uber’s official release does not state that exact figure.

## V. Models and Open-Source Ecosystem

### 10. Ant Group’s inclusionAI open-sources Ling-3.0 base models: ~124B total params, ~5.1B active per token (open source)

**Summary:** On August 20, Ant Group’s inclusionAI lab open-sourced the Ling-3.0 language base-model family on Hugging Face and related platforms, emphasizing sparse MoE efficiency and releasing multi-stage training checkpoints for continued pretraining, fine-tuning, and distillation research. Public specs put flagship Ling-3.0-flash-base / Ling-3.0-flash at about 124B total parameters with ~5.1B activated per token, hybrid-linear attention (KDA and Gated MLA), large expert routing, and a native context up to 256K; reports say the release is under an MIT license. Ant’s developer docs position Ling-3.0-flash as a high-throughput, lower-cost hybrid reasoning model and list a smaller Ling-3.0-tiny variant.

### 11. DeepSeek Harness ships first major public-beta update rc.8: native multimodal input plus Claude Code / Codex sub-agents (open source)

**Summary:** Zhidx reported on August 20 that DeepSeek’s open agent framework DeepSeek Harness released v0.1.0-rc.8, its first major update after the August 13 v0.1 public beta, with 14 changes. Highlights include native image requests and mixed image-text inputs so `/goal` and `/plan` can accept pictures; optional Claude Code and Codex profile bundles as sub-agents; improved Windows PTY/PowerShell persistent sessions; and fixes for oversized image failures, stream cancellation, and custom-gateway compatibility. Community testing also notes that for text-only models, Harness can fall back to OCR, color stats, and pixel scans, then feed structured evidence back to the LLM. The official GitHub release tag is `dsh-v0.1.0-rc.8`.
