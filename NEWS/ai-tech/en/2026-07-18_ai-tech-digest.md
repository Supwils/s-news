### 2. EU DMA orders: Google must open Android AI interoperability and share Search data (Regulation / Europe)
**Summary:** On July 16 the European Commission issued two binding DMA specification decisions for Google. Android must open about 11 system-level features to rival AI assistants—including voice invocation and in-app task hooks—via the next major Android release and no later than about August 1, 2027, with concurrent hotword detection due by about August 1, 2028. Separately, from about January 2027 Google must share anonymized Search data with eligible search engines and AI chatbots that offer search functions, aiming to blunt Google’s roughly 90% European search share. Google warned of privacy and security risks; the Commission said multi-layer anonymization and security checks are built in.

**Links:**

- [European Commission — Guidance on Android AI interoperability and Search data sharing](https://digital-markets-act.ec.europa.eu/commission-provides-guidance-google-ai-interoperability-android-and-sharing-google-search-data-under-2026-07-16_en)
- [The Verge — Google ordered to open Android and Search to rivals in Europe](https://www.theverge.com/policy/966438/eu-google-android-ai-interoperability-search-data-dma)

**Commentary:** Europe is unbundling the default assistant and search-data moat—Gemini’s Android distribution edge is being forced into swappable interfaces.
