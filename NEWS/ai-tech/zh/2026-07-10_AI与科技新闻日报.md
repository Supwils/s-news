## 一、政策与监管

### 1. 欧盟认定 **Meta** **Instagram**/**Facebook**「成瘾性设计」违反 **DSA**，或面临最高 **6%** 全球营收罚款（监管 / 欧洲）
**摘要：** 据欧盟委员会官网、**ABC News** **2026 年 7 月 10 日**报道，欧盟委员会当日发布初步认定，**Meta** 旗下 **Instagram** 与 **Facebook** 的无限滚动、自动播放、推送通知及高度个性化推荐等设计构成「成瘾性设计」，未能充分评估其对用户（尤其未成年人与脆弱群体）身心健康之风险，现有缓解措施亦不足以消除危害，违反《数字服务法》（**DSA**）。委员会要求 **Meta** 默认关闭自动播放与无限滚动、落实有效屏幕时间中断机制，并降低推荐系统对「参与度」的依赖；若最终确认违规，最高可处全球年营收 **6%** 罚款。
**链接：**

- [European Commission — Commission preliminarily finds the addictive design of Instagram and Facebook in breach of the Digital Services Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-addictive-design-instagram-and-facebook-breach-digital-services-act)
- [ABC News — EU demands Facebook and Instagram dismantle design features it calls addictive](https://abcnews.com/Technology/wireStory/eu-demands-facebook-instagram-dismantle-design-features-calls-134643678)

**简评：** 监管矛头从「内容审核」转向「产品设计本身」——若 **Meta** 被迫默认关闭成瘾机制，全球社交平台的增长公式将面临结构性改写。

**链接：**

- [Google Cloud — Solve harder problems with AlphaEvolve, now available to everyone on Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/alphaevolve-is-available-for-everyone)

**链接：**

- [South China Morning Post — Meet Biomni: the free powerful biomed AI agent turning data into hypotheses](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3359945/meet-biomni-free-powerful-biomed-ai-agent-turning-data-hypotheses)
- [Science — Autonomous biomedical research with an artificial intelligence agent (DOI: 10.1126/science.adz4351)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adz4351)
